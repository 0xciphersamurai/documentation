# Settlement - When Consensus Forms

The moment you've been waiting for: **payday**.

Unlike Polymarket where someone has to declare who won, Trigger Market settles automatically when the market reaches natural consensus.

**No oracles. No judges. Just math watching prices.**

## How You Know It's Coming

Markets settle when prices stop moving. Simple as that.

**The logic:** When everyone's had their say, made their trades, and adjusted their positions - prices stabilize. That stability IS the consensus.

The system watches for four things:

```
Settlement Criteria:
✅ Minimum Age: Debate at least 7 days old (prevents premature settlement)
✅ Stable Prices: Variance drops below threshold (prices stopped changing)
✅ Near Averages: Current prices match long-term averages (no last-minute manipulation)
✅ Confirmed: Stayed stable across multiple checks (sustained consensus, not temporary)
```

When all four hit green? **Market settles. Trading freezes. Payouts unlock.**

## What You Get

Dead simple: **Your shares × Final price = Your payout**

**Example:**
- You hold 500 shares of Opinion A
- Opinion A settles at $0.68 final price
- Your payout: 500 × $0.68 = **$340**

If you bought those shares for $200? **You made $140 profit (70% return).**

All opinions pay out proportionally. There's no single "winner" - every opinion pays based on its final price. You profit if you bought below the settlement price.

## The Timeline

**Week 1-2:** Active trading, prices moving, debates happening. Far from settlement.

**Week 3-4:** Patterns emerging, consensus forming, volatility decreasing. Getting closer.

**Week 5+:** Prices stable, little movement, community agreed. Settlement criteria start getting checked.

**Settlement hits:** Market freezes at current prices. Those are your final prices. Payout calculation is instant.

**Claiming:** Click "Claim Payout" button. Your USDC hits your wallet. Done.

## What Triggers It

The system checks settlement criteria automatically during trading. Every time someone trades, it checks: "Should this market settle?"

If yes → market freezes immediately. If no → trading continues.

**You can also manually trigger a check** via the "Update Settlement Status" button. Costs a bit of gas but lets you force a check if you think criteria are met.

## Prices Don't Sum to 1 (And That's Fine)

Quick note: Unlike Polymarket where Yes/No prices sum to 100%, Trigger Market prices don't sum to exactly 1.

**Each price is between 0 and 1** (so they look like probabilities), but the sum can be slightly more or less than 1.

**Why this doesn't matter:** At extremes, it behaves like you'd expect:
- When one opinion dominates at 0.95, others are near 0.05 - sum is ~1
- The system normalizes everything correctly for payouts
- You still get paid proportionally based on final prices

**Bottom line:** Don't overthink it. The math works. Your payout is fair.

## Maximizing Your Payout

**Buy low, hold through settlement:** Classic strategy. Find undervalued opinions, buy early, hold until prices stabilize higher.

**Track settlement progress:** When you see 3/4 criteria met, consider locking in profits on risky positions. Or double down if you think final prices will be even better.

**Don't trade right before settlement:** When markets are close to settling, prices are near their final values. Little profit left. High certainty, low reward.

**Best opportunities are early:** Weeks before settlement, prices are inefficient. That's when you can buy $0.30 opinions that settle at $0.65.

## The Beauty of Automated Settlement

**No waiting for oracles** to make decisions or collect data.

**No disputes** about who won or what happened.

**No trust required** in any third party.

**Just natural market dynamics** revealing consensus and paying you accordingly.

The market itself decides when consensus has formed. Prices tell the story. Settlement is just the period at the end of the sentence.

## What If Prices Never Stabilize?

Rare, but possible: a debate where consensus never forms. Prices keep fluctuating forever.

**In practice:** Debates eventually stabilize. Humans reach conclusions. Capital finds equilibrium. Markets mature.

**If it takes months?** That's fine. You can exit your position anytime. Sell and move capital elsewhere. No one's forcing you to wait for settlement.

**The system is patient.** It only settles when genuine consensus exists. If prices keep moving, that means the debate is still active and settlement shouldn't happen yet.

# Settlement Basics

Settlement is when a market concludes and final payouts are determined. Unlike traditional prediction markets that require oracles, Trigger Market settles based on price stability over time.

## What is Settlement?

**Settlement** is the final state of a market where:
- Trading stops permanently
- Prices lock at final values
- Payouts become claimable
- Consensus is recorded

**Not binary win/lose**: Every opinion pays out based on its final price.

**Example**:
Market: "Best JavaScript framework?"

**Final settlement prices**:
- React: 58¢
- Vue: 24¢
- Svelte: 12¢
- Angular: 6¢

**Payouts**:
- 100 React shares → $58
- 100 Vue shares → $24
- 100 Svelte shares → $12
- 100 Angular shares → $6

## Why Time-Based Settlement?

### The Oracle Problem

Traditional prediction markets need oracles to determine "who was right":
- **"Bitcoin hits $100K?"** → Check price feed
- **"Who wins election?"** → Check official results

**For subjective questions**, there's no objective answer:
- "Which framework is best?" → No oracle can answer
- "Should we fund this proposal?" → No right/wrong

### The Trigger Market Solution

**Replace oracles with consensus stability:**

**Logic**: If prices have been stable for an extended period, consensus has been reached.

**No external input needed**:
- Fully onchain
- Deterministic
- Trustless
- Works for any debate

## The Four Settlement Criteria

All four criteria must be met **simultaneously** for settlement to trigger.

### Criterion 1: Minimum Age

**Rule**: Market must be at least a minimum age (typically 7 days)

**Purpose**:
- Prevent premature settlement
- Ensure adequate debate time
- Allow information to emerge

**Why it matters**:
- Day 1: Prices highly volatile
- Day 3: Still discovering consensus
- Day 7+: Enough time for debate

**Example**:
- Market created: January 1
- Minimum age: 7 days
- Earliest settlement: January 8+

<Callout title="Cannot Settle Before Minimum Age" type="info">
No matter how stable prices are, markets cannot settle before minimum age. This ensures proper deliberation time.
</Callout>

### Criterion 2: Time-Weighted Variance

**Rule**: Historical price variance (time-weighted) must be below threshold T₂

**Formula** (simplified):
```
Variance = Weighted average of (Price - Mean)² over history
```

**Weighting**: Prices weighted by how long they persisted

**Purpose**:
- Ensure sustained stability
- Ignore brief spikes
- Detect true consensus

**Why time-weighting matters**:

**Without time-weighting**:
- Price at 50¢ for 1 second = same weight as 50¢ for 1 hour
- Brief manipulations count equally
- Easy to game

**With time-weighting**:
- Price at 50¢ for 1 second ≈ 0% weight
- Price at 50¢ for 1 hour = significant weight
- Only sustained prices matter

**Example**:
- Price history: 30¢ (1 day), 50¢ (5 days), 32¢ (1 hour), 48¢ (2 days)
- Time-weighted mean ≈ 47¢ (dominated by 50¢ and 48¢)
- Variance based on this weighting
- Must be below T₂ to pass

### Criterion 3: Instantaneous Variance

**Rule**: Recent price volatility must be below threshold T₃

**Formula**:
```
Instantaneous Variance = Recent price movements (last N blocks/hours)
```

**Purpose**:
- Confirm **current** stability
- Ensure no recent disruptions
- Prevent settling during active debate

**Why separate from Criterion 2?**:
- Criterion 2 looks at all history
- Criterion 3 focuses on **right now**
- Both must be stable

**Example**:

**Passes Criterion 3**:
- Last 24 hours: 52.1¢, 52.3¢, 51.9¢, 52.2¢
- Very low variance
- Currently stable ✅

**Fails Criterion 3**:
- Last 24 hours: 52¢, 48¢, 55¢, 50¢, 53¢
- High variance
- Currently volatile ❌

### Criterion 4: Age-Weighted Variance

**Rule**: Age-weighted variance must be below decreasing threshold

**Formula** (simplified):
```
Threshold = T₄ × (1 - age_factor)
```

**Purpose**:
- Makes settlement easier as market ages
- Recognizes diminishing returns of extended debate
- Prevents markets running forever

**Why it matters**:

**Young market** (7-14 days):
- High threshold required
- Hard to settle
- More debate time valuable

**Mature market** (30+ days):
- Lower threshold required
- Easier to settle
- Consensus likely formed

**Old market** (90+ days):
- Very low threshold
- Will settle soon
- Consensus stable

**Example**:
- Day 7: Threshold = 100% of T₄ (hardest to settle)
- Day 30: Threshold = 70% of T₄
- Day 90: Threshold = 30% of T₄ (easiest to settle)

## How Settlement Triggers

Settlement is permissionless:

<Steps>

### Criteria Check

Anyone can check if all four criteria are met:
- Read market state
- Calculate variances
- Compare to thresholds
- Determine if settleable

**Available onchain**:
- Current status of each criterion
- Progress toward thresholds
- Estimated time to settlement

### Settlement Transaction

If all criteria met, anyone can call settle:

```solidity
market.settle()
```

**What happens**:
1. Contract verifies all four criteria ✅
2. Market state changes to "Settled"
3. Final prices locked forever
4. Trading disabled
5. Payouts enabled
6. Event emitted

**Who can call?**:
- Anyone (permissionless)
- No special privilege needed
- Small gas fee (~$0.50)

**Incentive**:
- First to settle may receive small reward
- OR simply a public service
- Allows automation

### Post-Settlement

After settlement:

**Trading**:
- ❌ Completely disabled
- ❌ Cannot buy or sell
- ❌ Cannot propose opinions
- ✅ Can still view history

**Prices**:
- 🔒 Locked at final values
- 📊 Recorded onchain forever
- 📈 Historical data preserved

**Payouts**:
- ✅ Claimable immediately
- ✅ No deadline to claim
- ✅ Pro-rata based on shares × final price

</Steps>

## Settlement Probability

### Estimating Time to Settlement

Check criteria progress:

**All criteria under 80% of threshold**:
- Settlement distant (weeks away)
- Active price discovery
- Trade freely

**2-3 criteria met, others 90%+**:
- Settlement approaching (days)
- Prices stabilizing
- Consider exit strategy

**All criteria 98%+**:
- Settlement imminent (hours)
- Final positioning
- Lock in or exit NOW

### Factors Affecting Settlement Speed

**Accelerates settlement**:
- Low trading volume
- Stable prices
- Market aging
- Clear consensus

**Delays settlement**:
- High trading volume
- Price volatility
- New information
- Active debates

**Resets progress**:
- Major price movements
- Opinion replacement
- Significant news
- Large trades

<Callout title="Settlement Can Reset" type="warn">
Markets can get close to settlement, then have criteria progress reset if major price movements occur. This is intentional - it means consensus hasn't truly stabilized yet.
</Callout>

## Payout Mechanics

### How Payouts Work

**Calculation**:
```
Payout = Number of Shares × Final Settlement Price
```

**Example positions at settlement**:

**Trader A**:
- Holds: 500 AWS shares
- Final price: 53¢
- Payout: 500 × $0.53 = **$265**

**Trader B**:
- Holds: 800 GCP shares
- Final price: 26¢
- Payout: 800 × $0.26 = **$208**

**Trader C**:
- Holds: -300 Azure shares (short position)
- Final price: 14¢
- Must pay: 300 × $0.14 = **-$42**
- Had received when selling: ~$50
- Net profit: **+$8**

After settlement:

### Click "Claim Payout"

Initiates claim transaction

**Transaction details**:

## Settlement vs Selling Before

Traders face a key decision: Hold to settlement or sell before?

### Selling Before Settlement

**Advantages**:
- Realize profits immediately
- Avoid settlement uncertainty
- Redeploy capital elsewhere
- No risk of reversal

**Disadvantages**:
- Pay trading fees (1%)
- Price impact on exit
- May exit too early
- Miss final price appreciation

**Best when**:
- You need liquidity now
- Better opportunities elsewhere
- Nervous about reversal
- Settlement timeline unclear

### Holding to Settlement

**Advantages**:
- No exit fees
- No price impact
- Full final price payout
- Maximum profit potential

**Disadvantages**:
- Capital locked until settlement
- Risk of consensus reversal
- Uncertain timing
- Opportunity cost

**Best when**:
- High conviction in position
- Settlement approaching soon
- No immediate better opportunities
- Want maximum returns

### Hybrid Strategy

Many traders:
1. Sell partial position to de-risk (take some profit)
2. Hold remainder to settlement (max upside)
3. Rebalance as settlement approaches

**Example**:
- Hold 1000 shares at 35¢ avg entry
- Price reaches 55¢
- Sell 500 shares (lock in profit, fees paid)
- Hold 500 shares to settlement at 58¢
- **Result**: Profit taken + upside captured

## Anti-Gaming Mechanisms

### Why Time-Weighting is Critical

**Attack scenario**: Manipulator tries to prevent settlement

**Method**:
- Wait until all criteria nearly met
- Make large opposing trades
- Spike prices briefly
- Try to reset criteria

**Why it fails**:

**Cost**:
- Large trades pay 1% fees
- Significant price impact
- Capital required substantial

**Benefit**:
- Brief spike ≈ 0.1% weight in variance calculation
- Minimal impact on time-weighted criteria
- Would need sustained manipulation (very expensive)

**Result**: Economically irrational to attempt

### Settlement is Inevitable

If true consensus has formed:
- Prices will stabilize again quickly
- Criteria will be met eventually
- Manipulation only delays, not prevents
- Market settles when ready

## Key Takeaways

✅ **Settlement** = market concludes, prices lock, payouts available

✅ **Four criteria** must ALL be met simultaneously

✅ **Time-weighted variance** prevents manipulation via brief spikes

✅ **Age-based threshold** makes older markets easier to settle

✅ **Payouts** = shares × final price (all opinions pay out)

✅ **Permissionless** = anyone can trigger settlement when criteria met

✅ **No deadline** to claim payouts - funds held securely onchain

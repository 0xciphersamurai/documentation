# Your First Trade

Ready to take your first position on a consensus market? This guide walks you through the entire process, from finding a market to executing your trade.

<Callout title="Prerequisites" type="info">
Before you start:
- ✅ Wallet connected to Trigger Market
- ✅ On the Base network
- ✅ At least $10-20 worth of ETH for trading + gas
</Callout>

## Understanding the Trade Flow

Here's what you'll do:

1. **Browse markets** - Find a debate that interests you
2. **Analyze opinions** - Evaluate the competing perspectives
3. **Choose your position** - Decide which opinion to support
4. **Execute trade** - Buy or sell shares
5. **Monitor position** - Track your investment

Let's dive in.

## Finding a Market

<Steps>

### Navigate to Markets

Click "Markets" in the main navigation or visit the homepage.

### Explore Active Debates

You'll see a list of active markets. Each market shows:
- **Question**: The debate topic
- **Number of opinions**: How many perspectives are active
- **Total volume**: Amount traded so far
- **Time active**: How long the market has been running
- **Liquidity**: Available liquidity for trading

### Filter and Sort

Use filters to find markets that match your interests:
- **Category**: Tech, Business, Culture, Science, etc.
- **Volume**: High-volume (popular) vs low-volume (niche)
- **Age**: New markets vs established debates
- **Settlement status**: Active, approaching settlement, settled

### Read the Market Description

Click on a market to see:
- Detailed question description
- Market creator
- Creation date
- Settlement criteria status
- Full list of active and candidate opinions

</Steps>

Let's walk through a real example.

**Market Question**: "What's the best state management solution for React applications?"

**Active Opinions**:
- Redux (Price: 28¢)
- Zustand (Price: 35¢)
- Jotai (Price: 22¢)
- React Context (Price: 15¢)

**Current Analysis**:
- Zustand is leading at 35¢
- Redux has historical dominance but declining
- Jotai is gaining momentum in the community
- Context is seen as limited for complex apps

## Analyzing Opinions

Before trading, evaluate each opinion:

### Check Current Prices

Prices reflect current consensus:
- **Higher price (60¢+)**: Strong consensus supporting this opinion
- **Mid price (30-60¢)**: Competitive, debate ongoing
- **Lower price (0-30¢)**: Less support, but opportunity if undervalued

### Review Price History

Click "Price Chart" to see how consensus has evolved:
- **Uptrend**: Growing support
- **Downtrend**: Declining consensus
- **Stable**: Consensus has settled
- **Volatile**: Active debate, no clear winner

### Consider Trading Volume

Higher volume indicates:
- More liquidity (easier to trade)
- Higher confidence in prices
- Active community interest

### Read Opinion Descriptions

Each opinion has a description explaining:
- The core argument
- Key benefits
- Relevant context
- Proponent's rationale

### Analyze Time to Settlement

Check settlement criteria progress:
- **Criterion 1** (Minimum Age): Usually 7 days
- **Criterion 2** (Time-Weighted Variance): Stability of prices
- **Criterion 3** (Instantaneous Variance): Recent volatility
- **Criterion 4** (Age-Weighted Variance): Long-term stability

Markets closer to settlement have less price movement expected.

## Making Your Decision

### Strategy 1: Consensus Leader

**Goal**: Buy the emerging consensus leader before settlement

**Example**:
- Zustand is at 35¢ and trending up
- Community momentum is strong
- Buy at 35¢, expect settlement around 50-60¢
- **Profit**: 43-71% if correct

### Strategy 2: Undervalued Opinion

**Goal**: Identify undervalued opinion that will gain support

**Example**:
- Jotai is only 22¢ but has strong technical merits
- Recent React conf featured Jotai prominently
- You believe it's undervalued
- Buy at 22¢, target 40¢+
- **Profit**: 82%+ if community consensus shifts

### Strategy 3: Short the Overvalued

**Goal**: Sell (short) an opinion you think is overpriced

**Example**:
- Redux is at 28¢ but you believe it's declining
- Modern alternatives are better
- Sell at 28¢, expect drop to 15¢
- **Profit**: 46% if consensus shifts away

<Callout title="Risk Management" type="warn">
**Start small**: Your first trade should be 1-5% of your trading capital.

**Don't FOMO**: Don't rush into trades based on hype. Do your analysis.

**Diversify**: Don't put everything in one opinion or one market.
</Callout>

## Executing Your Trade

Let's buy Zustand shares in our example market.

<Steps>

### Select the Opinion

Click on "Zustand" to open the trading interface.

### Choose Buy or Sell

**Buy**:
- Acquire shares of this opinion
- Profit if price rises or settles higher
- You pay the current price

**Sell**:
- Sell shares you own (if you have any)
- Or short by selling shares you don't own
- Profit if price falls

For this example, select **Buy**.

### Enter Trade Amount

You can specify amount in two ways:

**Option A: By ETH Amount**
- Enter how much ETH you want to spend
- App calculates shares received
- Example: 0.1 ETH → ~285 shares at 35¢

**Option B: By Share Count**
- Enter number of shares desired
- App calculates ETH cost
- Example: 500 shares → ~0.175 ETH at 35¢

**Price Impact Display**:
The interface shows:
- Current price: 35.0¢
- Price after trade: 35.8¢ (example)
- Price impact: 2.3%
- Shares received: 285

<Callout title="Price Impact" type="info">
Larger trades move prices more. The AMM adjusts prices based on trade size.

**Tip**: For large positions, consider splitting into multiple smaller trades to reduce impact.
</Callout>

### Review Trade Details

Before confirming, verify:

**Trade Summary**:
- Opinion: Zustand
- Action: Buy
- Amount: 0.1 ETH
- Shares: ~285
- Current price: 35.0¢
- Execution price: ~35.4¢ (average)
- Price impact: 2.3%
- Est. gas fee: ~$0.05

**Fees**:
- Swap fee: 0.5% (0.0005 ETH)
- Market creator fee: 0.25% (0.00025 ETH)
- Opinion creator fee: 0.25% (0.00025 ETH)
- **Total fees**: 1% (0.001 ETH)

### Confirm Transaction

Click "Confirm Trade"

Your wallet will open showing:
- Contract interaction
- Total cost (trade + gas)
- Gas fee estimate

Click "Confirm" in your wallet.

### Wait for Confirmation

The transaction typically confirms in 1-2 seconds on Base.

You'll see:
- "Transaction submitted" (immediate)
- "Transaction confirmed" (1-2 seconds)
- "Position updated" (your shares are now in your portfolio)

</Steps>

### View Your Position

Navigate to "Portfolio" to see:

**Position Details**:
- Opinion: Zustand
- Shares held: 285
- Average entry price: 35.4¢
- Current price: 35.8¢ (already up!)
- Current value: 0.102 ETH
- Unrealized P&L: +0.002 ETH (+2%)

### Monitor Price Movements

Track your position:
- **Price chart**: See price movements over time
- **Market activity**: Monitor trading volume
- **Settlement progress**: Check criteria status
- **Community sentiment**: Follow discussions

### Set Price Alerts (Optional)

Some traders set alerts for:
- Price targets (e.g., notify if Zustand hits 50¢)
- Settlement imminent
- Large trades in the market

### Consider Your Exit Strategy

Think about when you'll exit:

**Strategy A: Target Price**
- Set a target (e.g., 50¢)
- Sell when reached
- Lock in profit

**Strategy B: Hold to Settlement**
- Believe in long-term consensus
- Hold until market settles
- Receive final payout

**Strategy C: Dynamic Management**
- Monitor market developments
- Adjust based on new information
- Sell partial position to de-risk

## Understanding Your Profit/Loss

### Unrealized P&L

While you hold the position:

**If price rises**:
- Entry: 35.4¢
- Current: 45.0¢
- Unrealized profit per share: 9.6¢
- Total: 285 shares × 9.6¢ = 27.36 ETH worth of profit
- Percentage: +27%

**If price falls**:
- Entry: 35.4¢
- Current: 28.0¢
- Unrealized loss per share: 7.4¢
- Total: 285 shares × 7.4¢ = 21.09 ETH worth of loss
- Percentage: -21%

### Realized P&L (After Selling)

When you sell or market settles:

**Selling example**:
- Bought at: 35.4¢ (285 shares for 0.1 ETH)
- Sold at: 45.0¢ (285 shares for ~0.128 ETH)
- Gross profit: 0.028 ETH
- Fees (1% × 2): -0.002 ETH
- **Net profit: 0.026 ETH** (+26%)

**Settlement example**:
- Bought at: 35.4¢ (285 shares)
- Settled at: 52.0¢
- Payout: 285 × $0.52 = $148.20 worth of ETH
- Initial cost: 0.1 ETH ($100 at purchase)
- **Net profit: ~48%** (assuming stable ETH price)

## Common First Trade Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Trading Too Large

**Problem**: Putting too much capital in first trade

**Solution**: Start with 1-5% of your capital to learn

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Price Impact

**Problem**: Large trades that move price significantly

**Solution**: Check price impact before confirming. Split large trades.

### Mistake 3: Not Understanding Fees

**Problem**: Surprised by 1% total fees eating into profits

**Solution**: Factor fees into your profit calculations from the start

### Mistake 4: Panic Selling

**Problem**: Selling at a loss due to short-term volatility

**Solution**: Have a plan before trading. Small drops are normal.

### Mistake 5: No Exit Strategy

**Problem**: Holding without a clear plan

**Solution**: Decide entry AND exit criteria before trading

### Mistake 6: Following the Crowd

**Problem**: Buying the highest-priced opinion because it's "winning"

**Solution**: Do your own analysis. The consensus leader might be overvalued.

## Advanced: Selling (Shorting)

You can also **sell** shares you don't own (shorting):

**How it works**:
1. Sell Zustand at 35¢ (receive ETH)
2. You now have a -285 share position
3. Price drops to 20¢
4. Buy back at 20¢ to close position
5. **Profit**: 15¢ per share

**Risks**:
- Price can rise indefinitely (but capped at 100¢)
- You profit when consensus shifts away
- Higher risk than buying

<Callout title="Shorting Tip" type="info">
Shorting is best when:
- You believe an opinion is clearly overvalued
- There's momentum shifting to alternatives
- You have strong conviction and analysis

New traders should focus on buying until comfortable with the platform.
</Callout>

# Add Your First Opinion

You're browsing a debate. Reading through the opinions. And suddenly you realize: **they're missing the obvious choice.**

The perspective everyone's talking about but isn't in the debate yet. The contrarian take no one thought to include. The new option that just emerged.

**This is your moment. Add it.**

## Why Add Opinions?

**Because the perspective matters** - If you see a gap in the debate, filling it improves the conversation for everyone.

**Because you'll earn from it** - If your opinion gets adopted into the main debate, you earn 0.25% of all trading volume in it. Your insight becomes income.

**Because you're building content** - The best debates have opinions users added, not just what the creator thought of. You're making debates better.

**Because it's easy** - Find gap → Write opinion → Stake $10+ → Done. If others agree, it graduates to the main debate.

## Spotting What's Missing

Browse active debates. Look for gaps:

**The hot new option** - A framework that launched recently. A protocol gaining buzz. It's not in the debate yet but everyone's talking about it.

**The contrarian view** - Debate is full of "A vs B vs C" but there's a growing "none of them are right" crowd. Add that perspective.

**The technical angle** - Debate is high-level but there's a specific technical solution deep circles know about.

**The obvious miss** - Sometimes debate creators just forget things. "Wait, how is X not an option?"

**Examples:**
- "Signals" in a React state debate (new framework, hot topic)
- "Build something new" in a governance framework debate (anti-framework take)
- "Taiko" in an L2 debate (new player, momentum building)

## The 5-Minute Add

### Step 1: Find Your Debate

Browse active debates. Click into one where you see the gap.

### Step 2: Check Candidate Opinions

Click "Candidate Opinions" tab. Maybe someone already proposed what you're thinking? If so, stake on theirs instead.

If not → Click "Propose Opinion"

### Step 3: Write Your Case

**Title:** Clear, concise name (e.g., "Signals.js" or "None - build new")

**Description:** Explain why this perspective matters. What makes it unique? Why should it be in the debate?

**Make it compelling** - Others need to understand and support it. Give context. Provide links. Make the case.

### Step 4: Stake Your Support

Minimum: $10 (typical, might vary)

**This is your backing** - Shows you believe in it. Starts the accumulation toward the threshold.

### Step 5: Promote It

Share with communities who care. "Just proposed [Opinion] in [Debate] - here's why it matters."

**Get supporters to stake** - Each stake brings it closer to graduating to the main debate. Build momentum.

## When Your Opinion Graduates

Once your candidate reaches the threshold (usually ~105% of the weakest active opinion's value), **anyone can trigger the graduation**.

**What happens:**
- Your opinion enters the main debate (the "big league")
- All stakes convert to tradeable shares
- Trading begins immediately
- You start earning 0.25% of all trades in your opinion

**Your shares:** If you staked early, you own a chunk. You can hold, trade, or sell them - separate from your fee earnings.

## The Content Creator's Edge

**You're not just trading** - You're shaping what debates include. Building the actual content of the platform.

**First-mover advantage** - Be first to propose hot new options. You own that opinion's economics.

**Community service** - Adding quality opinions makes debates better. People remember who contributes.

**Portfolio approach** - Propose opinions across many debates. Some flop. Some become huge. Expected value is positive.

## What Makes a Good Opinion?

**Distinct from existing options** - Not just "Option X but slightly different." Genuinely different perspective.

**Has a natural constituency** - Some group of people will naturally support this. Know who they are.

**Timely** - Catching momentum as it builds. Adding yesterday's hot take is less valuable than tomorrow's.

**Well-articulated** - Clear description. Good arguments. Links to evidence. Makes people go "oh yeah, that deserves to be in this debate."

## Common Questions

**"What if I propose and no one stakes?"**

Your $10 sits in the candidate pool. You can unstake it anytime. No big loss. Try proposing in more active debates next time.

**"What if someone proposes the same thing?"**

If multiple people propose similar opinions, the one with more stakes will graduate first. Or coordinate - stake on the better-written version.

**"Can my opinion get kicked out after entering?"**

Yes! Opinions can be replaced if they lose support. But you keep the fees you already earned, and it can come back later.

**"How many opinions should I propose?"**

Start with 5-10 across different debates. See what gets traction. Learn the patterns. Scale from there.

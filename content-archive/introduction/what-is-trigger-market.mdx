# What is Trigger Market?

**Trigger Market** is a fully onchain platform for creating and trading prediction markets on subjective debates - topics that don't have objective right answers.

## The Problem with Traditional Prediction Markets

Platforms like Polymarket and Augur work great for objective questions:

- "Who will win the 2024 election?"
- "Will Bitcoin hit $100K by December?"
- "Will the Fed raise rates next month?"

These markets work because:
1. **The outcome is objective** - there's a clear yes/no answer
2. **An oracle can verify it** - trusted sources determine what happened
3. **Everyone agrees on the result** - no ambiguity

But what about questions like:
- "Which programming framework is best?"
- "Should we fund this DAO proposal?"
- "What's the most promising AI research direction?"

**These have no objective answer.** Different people have legitimate disagreements. Traditional prediction markets can't handle them.

## The Trigger Market Solution

Trigger Market enables markets on **subjective debates** by replacing oracles with a novel settlement mechanism:

## How It Works (Simple Version)

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create a Market

Anyone can create a debate with initial opinions. For example:

**Market**: "Best state management for React in 2025?"

**Initial Opinions**:
- Redux
- Zustand
- Jotai
- MobX
</Step>

<Step>
### Trade on Opinions

Participants buy and sell shares of different opinions. Prices adjust based on demand:
- More buying → Price increases
- More selling → Price decreases

Prices satisfy the L² norm constraint: √(Σp_i²) = 1 (unit sphere geometry).
</Step>

<Step>
### Markets Evolve

New opinions can be proposed. If they gather enough support, they can replace weak opinions in the market. The debate evolves organically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Settlement via Stability

When prices stabilize (stop changing significantly), the market settles automatically. No oracle needed - mathematical stability = consensus.

Participants get paid based on final prices:
- Own 100 shares of "Zustand" at 0.68 (68%) final price → Receive $68
</Step>

</Steps>

## Key Innovations

### 1. Oracle-Free Settlement

Markets settle when prices remain stable for a specified period. **Price stability indicates consensus formation.**

The protocol uses four criteria:
- **Minimum age**: Market must be old enough (e.g., 7 days)
- **Low variance**: Prices must be historically stable
- **Current agreement**: Recent prices must match historical averages
- **Sustained confirmation**: Stability must persist across multiple checks

When all criteria are met → Market settles → Payouts distributed.

<Callout title="Why this works" type="info">
  When participants with real money at stake reach equilibrium, that *is* the consensus. Forcing further trading wouldn't change anything - the market has decided.
</Callout>

### 2. Dynamic Opinions

Unlike traditional markets where outcomes are fixed at creation, Trigger Market debates can evolve:

- Anyone can **propose new opinions** (costs nothing to create, requires stake to compete)
- Opinions accumulate **candidate stakes** while outside the main market
- When a candidate's stake exceeds the weakest active opinion's value (+ 5% threshold), **replacement** occurs
- Replaced opinions become candidates again - they can re-enter if support returns

This creates a **competitive marketplace of ideas** where the most compelling perspectives survive.

### 3. Elegant Mathematics

The system uses an **L² norm AMM** (Automated Market Maker) which provides:
- **Closed-form pricing**: Instant price calculation (no iteration)
- **Natural price bounds**: Prices stay in (0%, 100%) automatically
- **Convergence property**: As liquidity grows, prices become more stable

You don't need to understand the math to use the platform, but it's what makes oracle-free settlement possible.

<Callout title="For the technically curious" type="warn">
  The Hessian magnitude (measure of price sensitivity) decreases cubically with total liquidity. This means mature markets naturally resist price manipulation, creating conditions for stable settlement.

  Read more in [Market Dynamics](/docs/advanced/market-dynamics).
</Callout>

## Who is Trigger Market For?

### Traders & Speculators
Buy opinions you believe will win consensus. Early positions in winning perspectives yield profits.

### DAOs & Communities
Create markets to gauge collective opinion on governance decisions, funding priorities, or strategic direction.

### Researchers & Analysts
Aggregate expert opinion on technical questions, forecast trends, and identify emerging consensus.

### Market Creators
Launch debates on any topic and earn a percentage of all trading fees. Popular markets = passive income.

### Opinion Leaders
Propose compelling new perspectives in existing debates. If your opinion gets adopted, you earn fees on all trades.

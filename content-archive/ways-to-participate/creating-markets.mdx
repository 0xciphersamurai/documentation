# Creating Markets

Trading is active income. You work, you profit. Stop trading, income stops.

Creating markets is different. Create once, earn forever.

**This is how you build passive revenue in consensus markets.**

## The Opportunity

Every trade that ever happens in a market you created pays you a fee. Forever.

Create a market today. It runs for three months. Does $500,000 in total trading volume. You earn 0.25% of that: **$1,250** in fees. For creating one market.

Now imagine creating markets monthly. Each one generating fees for months or years. The income compounds. The revenue streams multiply.

**The early market creators are building permanent income streams right now.**

## What Makes A Great Market

Not every question becomes a good market. Great markets share specific characteristics.

**Compelling Debate**

The question needs to matter to a community. Not just "interesting" - people need to care enough to have opinions, discuss, and trade.

Ask yourself: Would this generate heated discussion in Discord? Would community members naturally take sides? Is there genuine disagreement?

**Multiple Valid Perspectives**

Binary questions are limiting. The best markets have 4-6+ plausible opinions. Enough diversity that the debate is rich but not so many that votes fragment meaninglessly.

**Clear But Subjective**

The question should be understandable in 10 seconds but impossible to answer objectively. "Best framework" is perfect. "Framework with most GitHub stars" is too objective.

**Accessible Yet Deep**

Beginners can understand the basic question. Experts can debate nuances. The market appeals to a wide range of participants.

**Timely Relevance**

Tap into current conversations. What is the community debating right now? What decision is approaching? What question keeps coming up?

Markets on dead debates generate no trading. Markets on live conversations generate volume.

## Finding Market Ideas

**Listen to communities.** Join Discord channels, read forums, follow Twitter discussions. What do people argue about? What questions recur? Where do opinions diverge sharply?

**Track debates.** Technical communities debate tools and approaches. DAOs debate strategy and resource allocation. Cultural communities debate values and preferences. Every community has its recurring questions.

**Watch for trigger events.** New protocols launch. Governance proposals emerge. Trends shift. Each creates fresh questions worth market-making.

**Notice gaps.** See an important debate with no market? Create it. First-mover advantage is real.

**Think meta.** Markets about markets. Markets about crypto trends. Markets about what makes good markets. Meta-questions can be fascinating and well-traded.

## Structuring Your Market

**The Question**

Make it clear, concise, and compelling. Frame it as something people want to answer.

Good: "What's the best state management solution for React apps?"

Bad: "An exploration of various technical approaches to managing application state in React-based JavaScript frameworks and libraries."

**The Description**

Expand on the question. Provide context. Explain why it matters. Set boundaries if needed.

This is your chance to frame the debate. Good framing attracts participants and generates trading volume.

**Initial Opinions**

Start with 3-5 obvious perspectives. These should be the natural starting points for the debate.

**Don't try to cover every possibility.** Leave room for community members to propose additional opinions. Market evolution is valuable.

**Write clear opinion descriptions.** Explain what each represents. Make the distinction between options obvious.

## The Economics of Market Creation

**Your revenue:** 0.25% of all trading volume.

**Example calculations:**

Market does $100K volume over its lifetime. You earn $250.

Market does $1M volume. You earn $2,500.

Market does $10M volume. You earn $25,000.

**What drives volume?**

High engagement. Controversial questions generate more trading than consensus ones.

Large communities. More potential participants mean more trading.

Long market lifetime. Markets that run for months accumulate volume.

Evolving debates. Markets where opinion proposals add new perspectives stay active longer.

**The beautiful part:** You don't need every market to be massive. Create 10 markets averaging $200K volume each? That's $5,000 in total fees. Create one monthly? That's steady income growth.

## The Launch Process

**Choose your question.** Something you're confident will generate interest and trading.

**Draft your opinions.** 3-5 strong initial perspectives that represent the major positions in the debate.

**Write compelling descriptions.** Both for the market overall and each opinion. Frame the debate to maximize engagement.

**Submit creation transaction.** On-chain, permissionless. No approval needed. Your market goes live immediately.

**Provide initial liquidity.** Seed the market with some capital so the first traders have liquidity. This isn't required but helps.

**Promote to communities.** Share in relevant Discord, Twitter, forums. Get the first traders engaged.

**Monitor and engage.** Comment on discussions. Answer questions. Help consensus formation happen.

## Promotion Strategies

Creating the market is step one. Getting traders to actually use it is step two.

**Target the right communities.** Technical market? Share in developer Discord. Governance market? Share in DAO channels. Reach people who care.

**Frame it as a conversation.** Don't say "I made a market." Say "Let's settle this debate once and for all" or "Time to put money where your mouth is on this question."

**Engage thought leaders.** If respected community members take positions, others follow. DM them directly. Make it easy for them to participate.

**Create content around it.** Write analysis of the question. Explain why each opinion has merit. Generate discussion that drives people to the market.

**Use the market itself.** Take your own position publicly. Explain your reasoning. Others will want to agree or disagree - in the market.

**Cross-promote.** Share on Twitter, Discord, Telegram, forums. Multiple touchpoints matter.

**Timing matters.** Launch markets when the community is actively debating the topic. Strike while interest is high.

## Building A Market Creation Practice

Don't create one market and hope it blows up. Create systematically.

**Launch regularly.** One market per week or month. Build a portfolio of fee-generating markets.

**Diversify topics.** Technical debates, governance questions, cultural discussions. Different markets appeal to different traders.

**Learn from each.** Track what works. Which questions generated volume? Which framing drove engagement? Refine your approach.

**Build reputation.** Become known as someone who creates great markets. Communities will start suggesting questions for you to create markets around.

**Reinvest fees.** Use market creation earnings to seed larger markets or provide liquidity. Compound your market creation business.

Here's what systematic market creation looks like over time:

**Month 1:** Create 4 markets. Average volume: $50K each. Total fees: $50.

**Month 12:** Have 48 markets, some settled but new ones launching. Strong reputation. Average volume: $500K. Monthly fees: $1,500.

And those Month 1 markets? Still generating fees from ongoing trading even a year later.

**This is how market creation becomes a business, not just an experiment.**

## Advanced: Market Series

Don't just create one-off markets. Create series.

**Weekly debates:** "Week 1: Best database for microservices." "Week 2: Best API architecture." "Week 3: Best deployment strategy." Each builds on the last. Community follows the series.

**Seasonal markets:** "Q1 2025: Most promising crypto trend." "Q2 2025: Most promising crypto trend." Recurring questions generate recurring trading.

**Tournament structures:** Multi-round debates where winning opinions from one market advance to face new challengers. Keeps engagement high across multiple markets.

**Linked markets:** Create multiple markets on related topics. Cross-promote them. Traders in one naturally discover others.

Series create audiences. Audiences create predictable volume. Predictable volume creates reliable income.

## Risks and Realities

**Not every market succeeds.** Some questions you think are fascinating generate zero interest. That's learning.

**Volume is unpredictable.** You might expect $100K and get $10K. Or expect $50K and get $500K. Estimation is hard early on.

**Promotion takes effort.** Creating the market is 20% of the work. Getting people to use it is 80%.

**Markets can be copied.** Someone can see your idea and create a similar market. First-mover advantage helps but isn't permanent.

**Quality compounds.** Your first markets might flop. Your tenth will be better. Your thirtieth will be systematically successful.

The early market creators aren't winning because every market is perfect. They're winning because they create consistently, learn systematically, and compound their understanding.

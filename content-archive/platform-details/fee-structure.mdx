# Fee Structure

Trigger Market uses a simple, transparent fee system that aligns incentives between traders, creators, and the platform. Understanding how fees work helps you make informed decisions about trading and creating.

## Total Fee: 1%

All fees are charged **only on exits** (selling shares, unstaking from candidates, and claiming settlement payouts). There are **no fees on entries** (buying shares or staking on candidates).

**Total fee**: 1% of the exit value

### Fee Distribution

The 1% is split three ways:

```
Exit Amount: $100
├─ Platform Fee: $0.50 (0.5%)
├─ Debate Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
└─ Opinion Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
```

**1. Platform Fee (0.5%)**
- Goes to the platform treasury
- Funds ongoing development and operations
- Initially **disabled** (0%)
- Can be toggled on by governance
- 7-day announcement required before enabling

**2. Debate Creator Fee (0.25%)**
- Goes to whoever created the debate market
- Earned on all trading in that debate, forever
- Initially **enabled** by default
- Can be toggled off by governance

**3. Opinion Creator Fee (0.25%)**
- Goes to whoever originally proposed the opinion
- Earned whenever that opinion is involved in a trade
- Tracked via immutable `opinionOriginalCreator[opinionId]`
- Initially **enabled** by default
- Can be toggled off by governance

## When Fees Are Charged

### ✅ Fees Apply To:

**Selling Active Shares**
- When you sell AMM shares back to the market
- Fee charged on the USDC you receive
- Example: Sell for $100 → Receive $99, $1 in fees distributed

**Unstaking from Candidates**
- When you withdraw stake from a candidate opinion
- Fee charged on the USDC you withdraw
- Example: Unstake $50 → Receive $49.50, $0.50 in fees

**Claiming Settlement Payouts**
- When you claim your payout after debate settles
- Fee charged on the payout amount
- Example: Claim $200 payout → Receive $198, $2 in fees

### ❌ No Fees On:

**Buying Active Shares**
- No fees when buying AMM shares
- Pay exactly the AMM cost

**Staking on Candidates**
- No fees when adding stake to candidates
- Your full amount goes into the pool

**Opinion Replacement**
- No fees on the replacement transaction itself
- Fees only apply when users later exit positions

## Fee Collection: Pull Pattern

Fees don't get sent immediately when they're generated. Instead, they **accumulate** in the smart contract and must be **claimed** by recipients.

### How It Works

```
Trade Happens → Fees Calculated → Accumulate in Contract
                                          ↓
                        Recipients Call claimFees() → USDC Sent
```

**Accumulated Balances:**
- Platform fees accumulate at address: `platformFeeRecipient`
- Creator fees accumulate per address: `claimableFees[creatorAddress]`
- All fees tracked onchain in real-time

**Benefits of Pull Pattern:**
1. **Gas Efficient** - One transfer when claiming vs three transfers per trade
2. **Resilient** - Blocklisted addresses can't break trading
3. **Batching** - Claim fees from multiple debates at once
4. **User Control** - Claim when you want, not forced

## Critical Deadlines ⚠️

### Fee Claims: 365 Day Deadline

<Callout title="Important: Fee Claim Deadline" type="warn">
**Fees from trading activity must be claimed within 365 days of debate settlement.**

After 365 days, unclaimed fees remain in the contract permanently and cannot be withdrawn.
</Callout>

**Timeline:**
1. Debate settles on Day 0
2. Fees from all historical trading are now claimable
3. You have 365 days to call `claimFees()`
4. After Day 365, unclaimed fees are locked forever

**Example:**
- Debate settles January 1, 2025
- You earned $150 in creator fees
- You have until January 1, 2026 to claim
- If unclaimed by then, the $150 stays in contract forever

**Who This Affects:**
- Debate creators (0.25% of all volume)
- Opinion creators (0.25% of their opinion's volume)
- Platform (0.5% if enabled)

### Payout Claims: NO Deadline

<Callout title="Good News: Payout Claims Never Expire" type="info">
**Settlement payouts (your shares × final price) have NO deadline.**

You can claim your payouts at any time after settlement finality (100 blocks). There is no rush.
</Callout>

**Why the difference?**
- **Fees** are creator earnings → deadline encourages active claiming
- **Payouts** are user's own capital → no deadline ensures users never lose their money

## Settlement Finality Period

After a debate settles, there's a **100-block confirmation period** before payouts and fees can be claimed.

**Why 100 blocks?**
- Ensures settlement is final and won't be reorganized
- Prevents claims during potential blockchain reorganizations
- Standard safety period (~20 minutes on Base)

**Timeline:**
```
Settlement Triggered (Block N)
    ↓
Waiting Period (Blocks N+1 to N+99)
    ↓
Finality Reached (Block N+100)
    ↓
Claims Now Available
```

After block N+100:
- ✅ Users can claim payouts (no deadline)
- ✅ Creators can claim fees (365-day deadline)

## Fee Examples

### Example 1: Simple Trade

**Setup:**
- You sell $500 worth of shares
- Platform fees: Disabled (0%)
- Creator fees: Enabled (0.5% total)

**Calculation:**
```
Exit Amount: $500
Platform Fee (0%): $0
Creator Fees (0.5%): $2.50
  ├─ Debate Creator: $1.25
  └─ Opinion Creator: $1.25

You Receive: $497.50
```

### Example 2: Settlement Payout

**Setup:**
- You have 1000 shares of "Option A"
- Debate settles with "Option A" at price 0.45
- All fees enabled (1% total)

**Calculation:**
```
Payout = 1000 × $0.45 = $450
Total Fees (1%): $4.50
  ├─ Platform (0.5%): $2.25
  ├─ Debate Creator (0.25%): $1.125
  └─ Opinion Creator (0.25%): $1.125

You Receive: $445.50
```

**Fee Recipients:**
- Platform gets $2.25 (claimable for 365 days)
- Debate creator gets $1.125 (claimable for 365 days)
- Opinion creator gets $1.125 (claimable for 365 days)

### Example 3: Multiple Opinions

**Setup:**
- You claim payouts from 3 different opinions in same debate
- Opinion A: 100 shares × 0.60 = $60
- Opinion B: 200 shares × 0.35 = $70
- Opinion C: 150 shares × 0.25 = $37.50

**Calculation:**
```
Total Payout: $167.50
Total Fees (1%): $1.675

Breakdown by Opinion:
Opinion A: $60 → Fee $0.60 (to A's creator)
Opinion B: $70 → Fee $0.70 (to B's creator)
Opinion C: $37.50 → Fee $0.375 (to C's creator)

Platform fee (0.5% total): $0.8375
Creator fees (0.5% total): $0.8375 (split among opinion creators)

You Receive: $165.83
```

Note: Each opinion's creator receives fees only from their opinion's payout, proportionally.

## Governance Control

The platform has two independent fee toggles:

**Toggle 1: Platform Fees**
- Currently: **Disabled** (0%)
- Can be enabled via governance vote
- Requires 7-day announcement before activation
- Once enabled, platform receives 0.5% of all exits

**Toggle 2: Creator Fees**
- Currently: **Enabled** (0.5% total)
- Can be disabled via governance vote
- If disabled, only platform fees apply (if enabled)
- Debate creators: 0.25%, Opinion creators: 0.25%

**Possible States:**
| Platform | Creator | Total Fee |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Off | Off | 0% |
| Off | On | 0.5% (current) |
| On | Off | 0.5% |
| On | On | 1.0% |

## Claiming Your Fees

### For Creators

**Check Your Balance:**
- View accumulated fees in your profile
- Shows breakdown by debate/opinion
- Updates in real-time as trades happen

**Claim Fees:**
1. Navigate to Profile → Earnings
2. See claimable balance for each debate
3. Click "Claim Fees"
4. Transaction sends USDC to your wallet
5. Can claim from multiple debates in one transaction

**Remember:**
- ⚠️ Claim within 365 days of settlement
- No gas fees if claiming zero (transaction reverts)
- Can claim partial amounts or wait to batch

### For Users (Payouts)

**Check Your Positions:**
- View all positions across debates
- See settlement status
- Calculate expected payouts

**Claim Payouts:**
1. Navigate to settled debate
2. See your final shares and prices
3. Click "Claim Payout" for each opinion
4. Or claim all opinions in one transaction
5. Receive USDC minus 1% fee

**Remember:**
- ✅ No deadline - claim anytime after 100-block finality
- Fees apply to payout amount
- Can claim from multiple opinions at once

## Fee Transparency

All fees are fully transparent and verifiable onchain:

**Track in Real-Time:**
- Every trade emits fee calculation events
- View accumulated fees per address
- Audit trail for all claims
- Historical fee earnings visible

**Verify Conservation:**
```
Total Deposits - Total Withdrawals =
    Active Vault Value
  + Candidate Pool Values
  + Unclaimed Fees
  + Unclaimed Payouts
```

Everything is accounted for. No hidden fees. No value creation or destruction.

## Why This Fee Structure?

**Aligned Incentives:**
- Debate creators want high-quality, engaging markets (more trading = more fees)
- Opinion creators want valuable, tradeable perspectives (trading volume = fees)
- Traders want fair, efficient markets (low fees encourage participation)
- Platform wants sustainable growth (fees fund development)

**Competitive Rates:**
- 1% total is comparable to other prediction markets
- 0.5% current rate (platform disabled) is very competitive
- No fees on entry encourages market participation
- Pull pattern reduces gas costs for everyone

**Governance Control:**
- Community can disable creator fees if needed
- Platform fees require announcement before activation
- Transparent, onchain voting for changes
- No surprise fee increases

<Callout title="Summary" type="info">
**Key Points:**
- 1% total fee on exits only (currently 0.5% with platform disabled)
- Fees accumulate and must be claimed via pull pattern
- **Fee claims: 365-day deadline after settlement**
- **Payout claims: NO deadline (claim anytime)**
- 100-block finality period before any claims
- Full transparency and governance control
</Callout>

## How It Works (The Essentials)

### **Debates Replace Events**

Traditional prediction markets: "Will X happen?"
**Trigger Market:** "Which approach is best?" - Subjective questions, real disagreement

### **Opinions Compete for Support**

**Big League (10 max):** Active opinions in the main debate, tradeable prices, visible
**Baby League (unlimited):** Candidates gathering stakes, waiting to enter

**The cycle:** Strong opinions enter big league. Weak ones get replaced. Replaced ones can return if they regain support. Natural selection for ideas.

### **Prices Reflect Collective Belief**

Each opinion has a price representing community support. Prices between 0-1, but don't sum to exactly 1 (L² norm math - don't worry, it works).

**What moves prices:**
- You backing an opinion → price rises
- Community loses faith → price falls
- Arguments win in comments → price adjusts
- External events → sentiment shifts

### **Settlement = Natural Consensus**

No oracle decides winners. Markets settle when prices stabilize naturally over time.

**Four criteria (all required):**
1. Debate at least 7 days old
2. Price variance below threshold (stability)
3. Current prices near historical means (no manipulation)
4. Sustained across multiple checks (genuine consensus)

**When settled:** Your shares pay out at final prices. Consensus recorded permanently onchain.

### **The Math Works (Trust It)**

You don't need to understand the formulas. The system is mathematically sound:
- Prices can't be easily manipulated
- Value is always conserved
- Settlement is fair and deterministic
- Everything verifiable onchain

**Built by people who know AMM design, consensus mechanisms, and blockchain security.** It works. Participate confidently.

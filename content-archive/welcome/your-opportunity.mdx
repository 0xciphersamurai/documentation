# Your Opportunity

You're reading this at a unique moment. Consensus markets are emerging as a new primitive for human coordination. The platform is live, markets are trading, but we're still in the earliest days.

**Early participants have outsized opportunities.**

## Four Paths to Success

Every successful platform creates multiple ways to win. Trigger Market is no different - but the opportunities here are uniquely diverse.

### 1. Trading: Spot Consensus Formation Early

The most direct path. See where consensus is forming before the crowd does. Buy low, sell high, or hold until settlement.

**What makes it special:** Traditional prediction markets require you to predict *objective outcomes*. Trigger Market requires you to understand *social dynamics* - how communities form consensus, how opinions shift, what captures collective attention.

It's a different skill. Some of the best traders won't come from DeFi or stocks. They'll come from community building, social networks, trend spotting. They understand humans, not just charts.

**Early advantage:** Right now, markets are thin. Price discovery is inefficient. Information asymmetries are massive. If you can read the sentiment of a technical community or understand what resonates with a DAO, you have serious edge.

The traders entering now are learning the patterns of consensus formation. This knowledge compounds. When larger markets launch, early pattern recognition gives permanent advantages.

### 2. Market Creation: Build Audiences and Earn Forever

Create markets that matter to communities you understand. Every trade that ever happens pays you fees. Forever.

**The opportunity:** Right now, no one knows what market formats work best. Which questions capture attention? What debate structures create engagement? How do you frame choices to maximize trading?

Early market creators are figuring this out. They're establishing themselves as the authorities on creating liquid debate markets. When this category explodes, they'll be the experts everyone seeks out.

**The economics:** 0.25% of all trading volume goes to the market creator. Permanently. Create a market that does $10M in volume over its lifetime? That's $25,000. And markets can stay active for months or years.

More important: you're building a brand. The successful market creators become known. They get invited to launch markets for DAOs, protocols, organizations. Market creation becomes a service, a skill, a reputation.

### 3. Opinion Proposing: Curate Perspectives

See a market missing a key perspective? Propose it. If it gets activated and people trade it, you earn fees on that opinion forever.

**Why it matters:** The best markets evolve. Initial opinions are often obvious, surface-level. The valuable insight is seeing what's missing. What perspective deserves to be in the conversation?

This is curation at its finest. You're not just proposing random options - you're shaping how communities think about questions. The best opinion proposers understand the intellectual landscape of their domains.

**Current state:** Most markets have 3-5 opinions. They're under-diversified. Entire categories of perspectives are missing. The opportunity for thoughtful opinion proposing is enormous.

As the platform matures, being known as someone who consistently proposes valuable, trade-worthy opinions becomes its own reputation. Communities will solicit your input when launching markets.

### 4. Early Adoption: Network Effects and Learning

Perhaps the least obvious but most important: just being here early.

**What you're learning:** How consensus markets work. What makes debates interesting. How to read sentiment in prices. How communities use these tools. This knowledge will be valuable for years.

**Network effects:** The participants here now are forming the core community. Relationships matter. Understanding emerges from shared experience. The people trading now will be the leaders, advisors, and power users when this category grows.

**Platform evolution:** Trigger Market will evolve based on early user feedback. Active early users shape the platform's direction. Your input influences the tools that millions might eventually use.

## What This Really Means

Let's be direct: most people reading this don't fully grasp what consensus markets enable. That's the opportunity.

When orderbooks launched, early traders made fortunes simply by *understanding* the mechanism before others. When AMMs launched, early liquidity providers earned extraordinary returns by *getting* the model early.

Consensus markets are another genuine innovation. Not an iteration - a new category. The people who understand it first win.

**But understanding takes time.** You can't just read the docs and "get it." You need to:

- Trade a few markets
- Watch how prices evolve
- See how communities engage
- Notice what creates consensus
- Observe settlement patterns

That learning happens through doing. The people doing it now are building intuition that can't be replicated by reading about it later.

## Why Now?

Three reasons this moment matters:

**1. Markets are still small**
You can influence prices with modest capital. Your trades matter. Information edges are huge. In six months, institutions might be here with serious size. Right now, individual insight can dominate.

**2. Mechanisms are still being discovered**
What makes a good market? How should opinions be structured? What drives liquidity? No one really knows. The patterns being discovered now will define best practices for years.

**3. The community is forming**
The core group of users, creators, and builders is coalescing right now. Being part of this early group means being part of the conversation that shapes the category.

## The Broader Picture

Step back further. What are consensus markets really about?

They're about improving human coordination on subjective questions. Every organization struggles with these. Every community debates them. Every movement needs to aggregate opinions.

If consensus markets work - if they become how groups make decisions on subjective questions - the addressable market is enormous. We're talking about a coordination primitive as fundamental as voting or markets themselves.

The people establishing themselves now aren't just early to a protocol. They're early to a new category of coordination technology.

## How To Think About It

Don't approach this as "trying out a new DeFi protocol." That's too narrow.

Think of it as:
- Learning a new way to read collective intelligence
- Building reputation in a new category
- Establishing yourself in a new ecosystem
- Getting edge in a new game

The financial opportunities are real and immediate. But the strategic opportunity - being known as someone who understood consensus markets early - may be more valuable long-term.

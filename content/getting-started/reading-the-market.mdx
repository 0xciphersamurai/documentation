# Reading the Market

Prices tell a story. Learning to read that story is how you make better decisions. This guide teaches you to interpret market signals.

## Price as Information

Remember: **Prices aggregate beliefs of everyone with capital at stake.**

When you see an opinion at 45%, that reflects:
- All the research people have done
- All the capital they've committed
- All the arguments they've evaluated
- Current collective belief

You're not just guessing—you're evaluating whether the market has it right.

## Reading Absolute Prices

### High Prices (50%+)

**What it means:**
- Strong consensus forming around this opinion
- Significant capital backing it
- Market believes this is most likely outcome

**Consider:**
- Is consensus deserved or herd mentality?
- What would change this view?
- Is there value in contrarian position?

**Example:**
Opinion A: 58%
- Clear leader
- Safe bet if you agree
- Limited upside (already priced in)

### Medium Prices (30-50%)

**What it means:**
- Viable contender, not dominant
- Significant but not overwhelming support
- Market sees real merit

**Consider:**
- Could this emerge as winner?
- What would push it higher?
- Good risk/reward if thesis develops

**Example:**
Opinion B: 42%
- Strong alternative
- Room to grow if momentum builds
- More upside potential than leader

### Low Prices (10-30%)

**What it means:**
- Minority view but present
- Some believers, not many
- Market skeptical but not dismissive

**Consider:**
- Why so low? Missing something?
- Or market missing something?
- High risk, high reward

**Example:**
Opinion C: 18%
- Underdog
- Could be undervalued dark horse
- Or could go to near-zero

### Very Low Prices (Below 10%)

**What it means:**
- Minimal support
- Market largely dismissive
- Vulnerable to replacement if candidate pool active

**Consider:**
- Is this legitimately weak?
- Or massively undervalued?
- Risk of opinion getting replaced

## Reading Price Changes

### Rapid Increase

**Pattern:**
30% → 35% → 42% (over days)

**Possible causes:**
- New information favoring this view
- Key community member backed it
- Arguments gaining traction
- Momentum building

**How to react:**
- Research WHY price is rising
- If fundamentals changed: trend might continue
- If just momentum: could reverse
- FOMO is dangerous—understand reason first

### Rapid Decrease

**Pattern:**
45% → 38% → 32% (over days)

**Possible causes:**
- New information contradicting this view
- Alternative opinions strengthening
- Support eroding
- Negative discovery

**How to react:**
- Understand the cause
- Has your thesis changed?
- If thesis intact: possible buying opportunity
- If thesis broken: consider exiting

### Stable Prices

**Pattern:**
42% → 42% → 43% → 42% (over weeks)

**Possible causes:**
- Market reached equilibrium
- No new information
- Positions established
- Approaching settlement

**How to react:**
- Less opportunity for trading gains
- Good for settlement holds
- Watch for breakdown signals

### Oscillation

**Pattern:**
40% → 45% → 38% → 43% → 39%

**Possible causes:**
- Market uncertain
- New information flowing
- Active debate ongoing
- Competing narratives

**How to react:**
- High volatility = high opportunity
- Can trade around oscillations
- Or wait for clearer picture

## Chart Patterns

### Uptrend

```
Price over time:
    /
   /
  /
 /
/
```

**Characteristics:**
- Higher highs, higher lows
- Sustained upward movement
- Volume often increasing

**Interpretation:**
- Building consensus
- Momentum with opinion
- Trend may continue

**Strategy:**
- Consider buying dips
- Hold existing position
- Take profits on spikes

### Downtrend

```
Price over time:
\
 \
  \
   \
    \
```

**Characteristics:**
- Lower highs, lower lows
- Sustained downward movement
- Support eroding

**Interpretation:**
- Consensus moving away
- Fundamentals weakening
- Trend may continue

**Strategy:**
- Consider exiting
- Or wait for reversal signals
- Don't try to catch falling knife

### Consolidation

```
Price over time:
_____
```

**Characteristics:**
- Price range-bound
- No clear direction
- Lower volume

**Interpretation:**
- Market digesting information
- Waiting for catalyst
- Could break either direction

**Strategy:**
- Wait for breakout
- Or accumulate if bullish
- Patience key

### Breakout

```
Price over time:
        /
_______/
```

**Characteristics:**
- Price breaks above resistance
- Often with volume spike
- New trend beginning

**Interpretation:**
- New information or momentum
- Previous equilibrium broken
- Could signal start of trend

**Strategy:**
- Confirm breakout with volume
- Consider entering on pullback
- Watch for false breakouts

## Volume Interpretation

### High Volume + Rising Price

**Signal:** Strong bullish momentum
**Interpretation:** Many people agreeing, adding capital
**Confidence:** High (confirmed by volume)

### High Volume + Falling Price

**Signal:** Strong bearish momentum
**Interpretation:** Many people exiting, losing faith
**Confidence:** High (confirmed by volume)

### Low Volume + Price Change

**Signal:** Weak move, low conviction
**Interpretation:** Few participants, could easily reverse
**Confidence:** Low (not confirmed)

### Declining Volume During Trend

**Signal:** Trend losing steam
**Interpretation:** Fewer new participants joining
**Watch for:** Possible reversal coming

## Holder Analysis

### Top Holders Tab

Shows who holds what:

**Large concentration:**
- Few holders own most shares
- Higher risk (could sell suddenly)
- "Whale" risk

**Distributed holdings:**
- Many smaller holders
- More stable
- True consensus

**New holders accumulating:**
- Fresh capital entering
- Possible new trend starting

**Old holders exiting:**
- Original believers leaving
- Possible top signal

## Activity Feed

### What to Watch

**Buying activity:**
- Who's buying?
- How much?
- Frequency?

**Selling activity:**
- Who's exiting?
- Why now?
- Position sizes?

**Patterns:**
- Same address buying repeatedly: high conviction
- Large sells: possible profit-taking or capitulation
- Many small trades: distributed interest

## Candidate Pool Signals

### Strong Candidate Accumulation

**Signal:**
Candidate approaching replacement threshold fast

**Interpretation:**
- New perspective gaining traction
- Current opinions might be inadequate
- Market composition could change

**Strategy:**
- Review the candidate opinion
- Is it actually better?
- Consider if your opinion vulnerable
- Or stake on candidate if compelling

### Stagnant Candidates

**Signal:**
Multiple candidates, none gaining momentum

**Interpretation:**
- Current active opinions adequate
- No compelling alternatives yet
- Stable composition

**Strategy:**
- Focus on trading active opinions
- Less concern about replacement

## Settlement Signals

### Approaching Settlement

Watch settlement progress box:

```
✅ Age: 15 days (required: 7)
✅ Stability: Low variance
✅ Agreement: Within 2%
⏳ Confirmations: 3/5
```

**When 3-4 criteria met:**
- Settlement likely within days
- Price movements limited
- Time to finalize positions

**Strategy:**
- Decide: hold to settlement or exit now?
- Less time for price to move significantly
- Lock in gains vs. wait for final payout

### Far from Settlement

```
✅ Age: 10 days
⏳ Stability: Moderate variance
❌ Agreement: Prices diverging
```

**Interpretation:**
- Still active debate
- Prices will continue moving
- More opportunity for trading

**Strategy:**
- More time to trade
- Can be patient
- Don't rush decisions

## Common Patterns to Recognize

### The Pump

**Pattern:**
Rapid price increase, often on news or hype

**Warning signs:**
- Too fast, not sustainable
- Volume doesn't support move
- No fundamental change

**Strategy:**
- Be skeptical
- Don't FOMO in
- Wait for confirmation or pullback

### The Dump

**Pattern:**
Rapid price decrease, often panic-driven

**Opportunity:**
- If fundamentals intact: buying opportunity
- If fundamentals changed: stay away

**Strategy:**
- Assess why it's happening
- Don't catch falling knife blindly
- Wait for stabilization

### The Rotation

**Pattern:**
Capital shifts from one opinion to another

**Watch for:**
- Opinion A down, Opinion B up (similar amounts)
- Suggests preference shift, not capital leaving
- New consensus forming

**Strategy:**
- Understand why rotation happening
- Follow or fade?

### The Consensus

**Pattern:**
Prices stabilizing around distribution that persists

**Characteristics:**
- Low volume
- Tight ranges
- Multiple confirmations

**Signal:**
Settlement approaching

**Strategy:**
- Finalize positions
- Prepare for settlement

## Combining Signals

Never rely on one signal. Combine:

**Price + Volume + Holder Distribution + Activity + Settlement Progress**

**Example bullish setup:**
- ✅ Price in uptrend
- ✅ Volume increasing
- ✅ New holders accumulating
- ✅ Positive news/arguments
- ✅ Far from settlement (time to develop)

**Example bearish setup:**
- ❌ Price in downtrend
- ❌ Volume on down days
- ❌ Large holders exiting
- ❌ Negative developments
- ⏳ Approaching settlement (limited time)

## Practice Makes Better

Reading markets is a skill developed through:

**Observation:**
- Watch multiple debates
- Note patterns
- See what works

**Experience:**
- Make trades
- See outcomes
- Learn from results

**Reflection:**
- Why were you right/wrong?
- What signals did you miss?
- What patterns emerged?

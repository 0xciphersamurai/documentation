# Join Your First Debate

This is where you stop lurking and start participating. Where your opinions become more than words. Where you back your beliefs with action.

**Let's get you into a debate that matters.**

## Finding Debates You Care About

The platform is full of debates. Some will excite you. Some won't matter to you at all. **Start with what you care about.**

Browse the active debates. Look for:

**Topics you have strong opinions on** - That framework debate where you *know* which one is better. That governance question where you've thought deeply about the tradeoffs.

**Communities you're part of** - Debates happening in spaces you participate in. Your DAO. Your tech community. Your research field.

**Arguments you've had before** - Those recurring debates on Twitter, Discord, forums. Now they're markets. Now you can put weight behind your position.

**Questions you're passionate about** - Not everything needs to be technical or serious. If you care deeply about something, that passion is valuable.

## Understanding What's Happening

Click into a debate. You'll see multiple opinions competing for support.

**Each opinion has a price** - This reflects how much the community currently supports that perspective. High price = lots of backing. Low price = less support (or not discovered yet).

**Opinions are arguments** - Read them. Some are detailed and thoughtful. Some are contrarian takes. Some are obvious choices everyone knows. Each represents a different way of thinking about the question.

**The debate is live** - Prices move as people join, change their minds, new information arrives. You're watching consensus form in real-time.

**Your job:** Decide which opinion you think deserves more support. Where you think the community will ultimately land.

## Three Ways to Participate

### 1. **Back an Active Opinion**

See an opinion in the debate that you believe in? You can support it directly by buying shares.

**What this means:** "I think this perspective is undervalued. The community will come to support this more than the current price reflects."

**You're betting on consensus** - Not predicting the future, but evaluating where collective opinion will land.

**Start small** - Your first position should be modest. You're learning the mechanics and testing your read of the community.

### 2. **Support a Baby League Opinion**

See the "Candidate Opinions" tab? These are perspectives gathering support to enter the main debate.

**What this means:** "I think this opinion deserves to be in the top discussion. It's not there yet, but it should be."

**You're early** - If your candidate makes it into the main debate, you were there first. Your stake converts to shares at ground-floor prices.

**Help it graduate** - Share it. Make the case. Get other believers to stake. When it hits the threshold, it enters the debate.

### 3. **Propose Your Own Opinion**

Don't see the perspective you believe in? **Add it yourself.**

Click "Propose Opinion" → Write your argument → Stake some support → Promote it.

**What this means:** "The debate is missing this angle. Here's why it matters."

**You're creating content** - This is how the platform grows. Users like you adding perspectives that debate creators didn't think of.

**You earn forever** - If your opinion makes it into the main debate, you earn 0.25% of all trading volume in your opinion. Your insight becomes income.

## The Human Element

Here's what makes this different from traditional trading:

**You're not predicting events** - You're reading people. Understanding social dynamics. Seeing where communities are headed.

**Domain knowledge matters more than finance skills** - Being deep in a community beats being good at charts. Understanding culture beats understanding candlesticks.

**Passion counts** - Strong beliefs, deeply held, often win out. If you care about something intensely, that conviction is an edge.

**Debates evolve** - Opinions can enter and leave the main debate based on support. The conversation grows and adapts. Nothing is fixed.

## After You Join

You're in. You've taken a position. Now what?

**Watch the debate unfold** - Check back regularly. See how prices move. Read comments. Notice what's gaining traction.

**Engage in discussion** - Comment on the debate. Explain your position. Debate others. This is as much conversation as it is trading.

**Adjust your conviction** - New information arrives? Change your position. Double down if you're more certain. Exit if you've lost faith. You're never locked in.

**See consensus form** - Over time, patterns emerge. Some opinions gain steam. Others fade. Eventually, prices stabilize and the debate settles. That's consensus.

## It's About More Than Money

Yes, you can profit if your read is correct. But that's not the only reason to participate.

**You're shaping consensus** - Your participation literally moves prices. You're part of forming collective opinion.

**You're defending what you believe** - This is your chance to back your arguments with real stake. Puts your money where your mouth is.

**You're building the platform** - Every debate you join, every opinion you support, every perspective you add - you're creating the content that makes this valuable.

**You're part of something new** - Consensus markets are genuinely novel. You're early to a new way of forming collective decisions.

## Common First-Timer Questions

**"What if I'm wrong?"**

Then you lose the money you staked. That's the trade-off for having your voice amplified. But the loss is limited to what you put in.

**"When does it end?"**

When the debate reaches natural consensus and prices stabilize. Could be weeks, could be months. The market itself decides.

**"Can I change my mind?"**

Anytime. You can exit your position, switch to a different opinion, or walk away entirely. You're never locked in.

**"What if I just want to watch?"**

That's fine too! Read debates. See how consensus forms. Learn the patterns. Participate when you're ready.

# Connect Your Wallet

Trigger Market is a fully onchain application running on Base. To interact with the platform, you'll need a Web3 wallet configured for the Base network.

<Callout title="First Time with Web3?" type="info">
Don't worry - we'll walk you through everything. Setting up a wallet takes about 5 minutes and is completely free.
</Callout>

## Supported Wallets

Trigger Market supports all major Web3 wallets:

### MetaMask
The most popular Web3 wallet with over 30 million users worldwide.
- **Platform**: Browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge)
- **Mobile**: iOS and Android apps available
- **Best for**: First-time users and experienced traders

### Coinbase Wallet
Official wallet from Coinbase, with excellent mobile support.
- **Platform**: Browser extension and mobile apps
- **Mobile**: Top-tier iOS and Android experience
- **Best for**: Users already on Coinbase

### WalletConnect
Connect with any of 100+ compatible wallets.
- **Platform**: Works with mobile wallets via QR code
- **Supports**: Rainbow, Trust Wallet, Argent, and many more
- **Best for**: Mobile-first users

## Setting Up MetaMask (Recommended for Beginners)

<Steps>

### Install MetaMask Extension

1. Visit [metamask.io](https://metamask.io)
2. Click "Download" and select your browser
3. Install the browser extension
4. Pin it to your toolbar for easy access

### Create Your Wallet

1. Click "Create a new wallet"
2. Create a strong password (you'll use this to unlock MetaMask)
3. **IMPORTANT**: Write down your Secret Recovery Phrase
   - 12 words in a specific order
   - **Never share this with anyone**
   - **Store it somewhere safe offline**
   - This is the ONLY way to recover your wallet

<Callout title="Security Critical" type="warn">
Your Secret Recovery Phrase is like the master key to your funds. Anyone with this phrase can access your wallet. **Never** share it, store it digitally, or enter it on any website except MetaMask itself.
</Callout>

### Confirm Your Recovery Phrase

MetaMask will ask you to confirm your recovery phrase by selecting words in the correct order. This ensures you've written it down correctly.

### Wallet Created!

You now have a Web3 wallet with a unique Ethereum address (starts with "0x").

</Steps>

## Adding the Base Network

Trigger Market runs on **Base**, an Ethereum Layer 2 network that offers:
- Fast transactions (1-2 seconds)
- Low fees (typically $0.01-0.10)
- Full Ethereum security
- Built by Coinbase

<Steps>

### Visit Trigger Market

Go to [triggermarket.io](https://triggermarket.io)

### Click "Connect Wallet"

The app will detect that you're not on the Base network.

### Approve Network Switch

MetaMask will prompt you to:
1. Add the Base network
2. Switch to Base

Click "Approve" and "Switch network"

**Network details** (added automatically):
- Network Name: Base
- RPC URL: https://mainnet.base.org
- Chain ID: 8453
- Currency Symbol: ETH
- Block Explorer: https://basescan.org

</Steps>

<Callout title="Manual Network Addition" type="info">
If the automatic prompt doesn't appear:
1. Open MetaMask
2. Click the network dropdown (top center)
3. Select "Add network"
4. Click "Add a network manually"
5. Enter the Base network details above
</Callout>

## Getting ETH on Base

To trade on Trigger Market, you need ETH on the Base network.

### Option 1: Bridge from Ethereum (If you have ETH on mainnet)

<Steps>

### Visit the Base Bridge

Go to [bridge.base.org](https://bridge.base.org)

### Connect Your Wallet

Connect the same wallet you'll use for Trigger Market.

### Enter Amount

Enter how much ETH you want to bridge from Ethereum mainnet to Base.

**Recommendation**: Start with $50-200 worth of ETH
- Trading: $20-100 for initial positions
- Gas fees: $5-20 for dozens of transactions
- Buffer: Extra for flexibility

### Confirm Transaction

1. Review the amount and fees
2. Click "Deposit"
3. Confirm in MetaMask
4. Wait 5-10 minutes for the bridge to complete

</Steps>

### Option 2: Buy Directly on Base (Easiest for new users)

<Steps>

### Use MetaMask Buy Feature

1. Open MetaMask
2. Make sure you're on the Base network
3. Click "Buy"
4. Select your payment method (card, bank, etc.)
5. Choose amount and complete purchase

### Use Coinbase (If you have a Coinbase account)

1. Buy ETH on Coinbase
2. Withdraw to "Base network"
3. Paste your wallet address
4. Confirm withdrawal - arrives in 1-2 minutes

</Steps>

### Option 3: Receive from Another Wallet

If someone sends you ETH:
1. Copy your wallet address from MetaMask
2. **Make sure they send on the Base network**
3. Share your address
4. Funds arrive in seconds

<Callout title="Network Critical" type="warn">
Always verify the sender is using the **Base network**. Sending ETH on the wrong network can result in lost funds.
</Callout>

## Connecting to Trigger Market

<Steps>

### Visit the App

Navigate to [triggermarket.io](https://triggermarket.io)

### Click "Connect Wallet"

Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right.

### Select Your Wallet

Choose your wallet provider:
- MetaMask
- Coinbase Wallet
- WalletConnect (for mobile wallets)

### Approve Connection

Your wallet will open and ask you to approve the connection.

**What this does:**
- ✅ Lets Trigger Market see your address
- ✅ Lets you sign transactions
- ❌ Does NOT let the app access your funds automatically

Click "Connect" or "Approve"

### Verify Connection

You should now see:
- Your wallet address (shortened, like "0x1234...5678")
- Your ETH balance on Base
- "Connected" status

</Steps>

## Understanding Wallet Interactions

When you use Trigger Market, your wallet will prompt you to approve various actions:

### Transaction Confirmations

**Every action that changes blockchain state requires confirmation:**
- Buying shares
- Selling shares
- Creating markets
- Proposing opinions
- Claiming rewards

**What you'll see:**
- Action description
- Gas fee estimate
- Total cost
- "Reject" or "Confirm" buttons

### Signature Requests

**Some actions only require signatures (no gas fees):**
- Viewing your trading history
- Accessing detailed analytics
- Certain data queries

**What you'll see:**
- Message to sign
- No gas fee
- "Cancel" or "Sign" buttons

<Callout title="Security Tip" type="info">
Always read transaction prompts carefully. Verify:
- The action matches what you intended
- The amount seems correct
- The gas fee is reasonable
- You recognize the contract address
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### "Wrong Network" Warning

**Problem**: Your wallet is connected to a different network.

**Solution**:
1. Open your wallet
2. Click the network dropdown
3. Select "Base"
4. Refresh the Trigger Market page

### "Insufficient Funds for Gas"

**Problem**: You don't have enough ETH to pay transaction fees.

**Solution**:
1. Check your ETH balance on Base
2. Bridge or buy more ETH (see "Getting ETH on Base" above)
3. Try the transaction again

### Wallet Not Connecting

**Problem**: The connection modal doesn't appear or hangs.

**Solution**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Make sure your wallet extension is unlocked
3. Try a different browser
4. Clear browser cache
5. Update your wallet extension

### Transaction Stuck "Pending"

**Problem**: Transaction shows "pending" for a long time.

**Solution**:
1. Wait 2-3 minutes - Base is fast but not instant
2. Check [basescan.org](https://basescan.org) with your transaction hash
3. If truly stuck (rare), try increasing gas in wallet settings

## Security Best Practices

### Never Share Your Secret Phrase

Your 12-word recovery phrase is the master key to your wallet. **Never** share it with anyone, including:
- Trigger Market team
- Support channels
- Social media
- Any website

**Trigger Market will NEVER ask for your recovery phrase.**

### Verify URLs

Always check you're on the correct website:
- ✅ `triggermarket.io`
- ❌ `triggermarket.com` (fake)
- ❌ `trigger-market.io` (fake)
- ❌ `triggermarket.org` (fake)

**Tip**: Bookmark the official site to avoid phishing.

### Review Transaction Details

Before confirming any transaction:
1. Read the action description
2. Verify the amount
3. Check the gas fee is reasonable
4. Confirm you initiated this action

### Use a Hardware Wallet (Advanced)

For larger amounts, consider a hardware wallet like:
- Ledger
- Trezor

These store your keys on a physical device, providing maximum security.

### Keep Software Updated

Regularly update:
- Your wallet extension
- Your browser
- Your operating system

Updates often include important security patches.

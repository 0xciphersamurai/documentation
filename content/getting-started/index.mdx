# Quick Start

Let's get you trading on your first consensus market. This guide assumes no prior experience with crypto markets or DeFi.

## What You'll Need

**Required:**
- A crypto wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect, etc.)
- Some USDC on Base network (the stablecoin used for all trades)
- ~$20 to start (minimum trades are flexible, but this gives room to experiment)

**Optional:**
- Base ETH for gas fees (~$2-5 to cover multiple transactions)

<Callout title="New to Crypto?" type="info">
Don't have a wallet yet? MetaMask is beginner-friendly: [Install MetaMask](https://metamask.io/)

Need USDC on Base? You can bridge from Ethereum or buy directly through exchanges that support Base.
</Callout>

## Your First Trade in 5 Steps

<Steps>

### Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit **trigger.market** (placeholder—actual URL)
2. Click **"Connect Wallet"** in the top right
3. Select your wallet provider (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.)
4. Approve the connection request in your wallet
5. Sign the message to verify ownership (no gas cost)

**You should see:**
- Your wallet address in the top bar
- Your USDC balance
- Your portfolio value (starts at $0)

<Step>
### Find a Debate

**Browse the home page** to find active debates:

**Filter by category:**
- Technology
- Governance
- Economics
- Science
- Culture

**Sort by:**
- Volume (most traded)
- Liquidity (most capital)
- New (recently created)
- Ending soon (close to settlement)

**What to look for:**
- Topics you understand or care about
- Active debates with recent trading
- Multiple opinions with distributed prices (no single dominant winner yet)

**Click on a debate** that interests you to see details.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review the Debate

On the debate detail page, you'll see:

**Current Opinion Prices:**
- Opinion A: 52%
- Opinion B: 45%
- Opinion C: 38%
- Opinion D: 30%

**Remember:** Prices don't sum to 100%. They satisfy √(Σp²) = 1 (L² norm)

**Review:**
- **Description**: What's being debated?
- **Opinion details**: Click each to read full arguments
- **Price chart**: How have prices evolved?
- **Comments**: What's the community discussion?
- **Top holders**: Who's backing which opinions?

**Settlement progress** shows how close to settling:
```
✅ Age: 12 days (required: 7)
⏳ Stability: Moderate (still trading actively)
```
</Step>

<Step>
### Make Your Trade

**Choose an opinion** you believe is undervalued or will gain consensus:

1. **Click "Buy"** on that opinion (or use trading panel on right)
2. **Enter amount** in USDC (e.g., $20)
3. **Review preview:**
   - Current price: 38%
   - Estimated new price: 39.5%
   - Shares you'll receive: ~51.2
   - Your position value: ~$20.00
   - Gas cost: ~$0.10

4. **Click "Confirm Trade"**
5. **Approve in your wallet** (sign transaction)
6. **Wait for confirmation** (~2 seconds on Base)

**Done!** Your position now appears in your profile.
</Step>

<Step>
### Monitor Your Position

**Check your profile** (click avatar → "Positions"):

**You'll see:**
- Debate name
- Opinion you hold
- Quantity: 51.2 shares
- Current value: $20.15 (if price rose slightly)
- P&L: +$0.15 (+0.75%)

**Watch the debate:**
- Price changes as others trade
- Your position value adjusts automatically
- Green = profit, Red = loss

**You can:**
- **Sell anytime**: Convert back to USDC (minus 1% fee if enabled)
- **Buy more**: Increase your position
- **Hold until settlement**: Claim final payout based on final price
</Step>

## Understanding Your First Position

### What You Own

You own **shares** in that opinion. Specifically:

- **Shares**: 51.2 (fixed quantity)
- **Current price**: 39% (changes with trading)
- **Position value**: Shares × Price = 51.2 × 0.39 = ~$20

### How Value Changes

**Price increases** (38% → 45%):
- Position value: 51.2 × 0.45 = $23.04
- P&L: +$3.04 (+15.2%)

**Price decreases** (38% → 32%):
- Position value: 51.2 × 0.32 = $16.38
- P&L: -$3.62 (-18.1%)

Your profit or loss depends entirely on price movement.

### When to Sell

**Consider selling if:**
- Price rose significantly and you want to lock gains
- You've changed your mind about the opinion
- You need the capital elsewhere
- Settlement approaching and you want certainty

**Consider holding if:**
- You believe price will rise further
- Opinion gaining momentum
- You want to see final settlement
- Transaction fees not worth it yet

### Settlement Payout

If you hold until settlement:

**Your final payout = Shares × Final price**

**Example:**
- You hold: 51.2 shares
- Final price: 0.42
- Gross payout: 51.2 × 0.42 = $21.50
- Minus 1% fee (if enabled): $21.29
- Your profit: $1.29 on $20 trade

## Common First-Time Questions

**Q: Can I lose more than I invested?**

A: No. Maximum loss is your initial investment (if price goes to near-zero). You can't lose more than you put in.

**Q: When should I trade vs. when should I wait?**

A: Trade when you have conviction. Waiting is fine if you're still researching or if prices don't reflect value you see.

**Q: How do I know if I'm overpaying?**

A: Watch "price impact" in the preview. Large trades relative to liquidity = more slippage. Start small to learn.

**Q: What if the debate settles before I sell?**

A: You receive payout based on final price. No deadline to claim. Might be higher or lower than current price depending on final settlement.

**Q: Can I trade multiple opinions in the same debate?**

A: Yes! You can hold positions in different opinions. They compete, so one rising usually means others falling.

**Q: What happens to my position if the opinion gets replaced?**

A: Your shares convert to candidate stake automatically (proportional claims preserved). You can unstake or wait for potential re-entry.

## Tips for New Traders

### Start Small

Don't bet your entire budget on first trade. Split across multiple positions to learn:
- How prices move
- How your intuition performs
- What info matters

### Read Before Trading

Review:
- Full opinion descriptions (click to expand)
- Comments from community
- Price history charts
- Who's holding what

Information matters.

### Watch Settlement Progress

Debates close to settling have less time for prices to move. Early-stage debates have more volatility and opportunity.

### Learn from Each Trade

After each position:
- Why did you buy?
- What happened to price?
- What would you do differently?

The market teaches through experience.

### Engage with Community

Use the comments:
- Ask questions
- Share your reasoning
- Learn from others' perspectives

Better understanding → better trading decisions.

# Getting Started

This section will guide you through your first steps on Trigger Market—from making your first trade to understanding your positions.

## What You'll Learn

- **Quick Start** — Make your first trade in 5 minutes
- **Understanding Your Position** — How to track and manage your holdings
- **Reading the Market** — Interpreting prices, charts, and market signals

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:

- A Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.)
- Some USDC on Polygon network
- A few dollars of POL for gas fees

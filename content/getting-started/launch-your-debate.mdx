# Launch Your First Debate

You've been thinking about a question. A debate happening in your community. A decision your DAO is facing. A topic people keep arguing about but never resolving.

**Turn it into a debate. Right now.**

## Why Create a Debate?

**Because the question matters** - If you care about a topic, if it's being debated anyway, why not give it a proper venue?

**Because you'll earn from it** - Every trade in your debate earns you 0.25% in fees. Forever. Popular debates become passive income.

**Because you're building the platform** - The debates that exist are the ones users create. You're creating the content others participate in.

**Because it's easy** - 5 minutes from idea to live debate. No approval needed. No gatekeepers.

## What Makes a Good Debate?

The best debates share these traits:

**People already argue about it** - Don't invent debates. Find ones happening naturally and give them a market.

**Multiple valid perspectives exist** - If there's an obvious right answer, it's not a debate. You want genuine disagreement.

**A community cares** - Even if it's niche, if a specific group cares intensely, that's enough. Passionate small communities beat indifferent large ones.

**It's timely** - Hot topics get more participation. Catch waves as they're forming.

**Examples:**
- "Best React state library 2025" (dev community, recurring debate)
- "Should the DAO fund Proposal X or Y?" (governance, real stakes)
- "Which AI safety approach matters most?" (research, passionate)
- "Ethereum L2 rankings Q1" (crypto, constantly debated)

## The 5-Minute Launch

### Step 1: Frame Your Question

**Make it clear and neutral** - "Best X for Y?" or "Which approach to Z?"

Bad: "Why X is better than Y" (biased)
Good: "X vs Y vs Z: Which is best?"

Bad: "What do you think?" (too vague)
Good: "Best DeFi protocol for yield: 2025"

### Step 2: Pick 3-10 Starting Opinions

You don't need to be comprehensive. Just seed the debate with the main contenders:

**For framework debates:** The popular ones everyone knows
**For governance:** The main proposals being discussed
**For cultural questions:** The common positions

**Others can add more later** - That's what candidate opinions are for. You're starting it, not defining it forever.

### Step 3: Add $50+ Liquidity

Minimum is $50 USDC (on Base, so gas is cheap).

**Where it goes:** Split equally across all starting opinions. Creates the initial market.

**What you get:** Shares proportional to your liquidity. You own part of each opinion. You can trade or hold them.

**More is better:** Higher liquidity = smoother trading = more participants = more fees for you.

### Step 4: Hit Deploy

Connect wallet → Confirm transaction → Wait 2 seconds.

**Done.** Your debate is live at its own URL. Trading is active immediately.

## After You Launch

**Promote it** - Share in relevant communities. "Just launched [Debate] on Trigger Market - come stake your position."

**Seed discussion** - Add the first few comments. Explain each opinion. Give context.

**Engage participants** - Answer questions. Acknowledge good arguments. Be present.

**Watch it grow** - See opinions get added. Watch consensus form. Earn fees on every trade.

## The Content Creator Mindset

**You're not just launching a market** - You're creating a venue for your community to express beliefs and form consensus.

**Think like a curator** - What debates deserve markets? What questions would your community love to trade on?

**Quality over quantity** - One great debate beats ten mediocre ones. Launch debates you actually care about.

**Build a series** - "Best X Q1 2025", "Best X Q2 2025", etc. People return for updates. You build a brand.

## Common Questions

**"What if no one participates?"**

Some debates flop. That's fine. Create 10 debates, 3 will hit. That's the game. Low cost to try (just gas), unlimited upside if it works.

**"How do I get people to join?"**

Share where your target community hangs out. Discord, Twitter, forums. If the topic is relevant, they'll come.

**"Can I create multiple debates?"**

As many as you want. No limits. Some creators launch multiple per week.

**"What if my debate is too niche?"**

Niche is fine if the niche cares intensely. 100 passionate participants beats 1000 casual ones.

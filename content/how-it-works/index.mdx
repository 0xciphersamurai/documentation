# How the System Works

This section covers the technical details of how Trigger Market operates—from settlement to fees to the mathematical guarantees.

## What You'll Learn

- **Settlement Process** — How debates conclude and payouts are calculated
- **Fee Structure** — Understanding exit fees, creator fees, and platform fees
- **Opinion Replacement** — The mechanics of how opinions enter and exit
- **Value Conservation** — The mathematical guarantee that funds are never lost

## Core Guarantees

The system is built on mathematical guarantees:

1. **Value Conservation** — Total deposited funds always equal total claimable funds
2. **Permissionless Settlement** — Anyone can trigger settlement when criteria are met
3. **Proportional Claims** — Your share of the vault is always preserved

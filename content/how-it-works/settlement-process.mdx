# Settlement Process

Every consensus market eventually settles. Understanding this process helps you know what to expect and how to claim your payouts.

## The Settlement Journey

### From Active to Settled

A debate goes through several stages:

**1. Active Trading**
- Prices moving
- Positions changing
- Discussion ongoing
- No consensus yet

**2. Convergence**
- Prices stabilizing
- Fewer trades
- Positions established
- Criteria being met

**3. Settlement Trigger**
- All 4 criteria met
- Market freezes immediately
- No more trading
- Final prices locked

**4. Finality Period**
- 100-block confirmation (~20 minutes)
- Safety buffer for blockchain finality
- Ensures settlement won't be reversed

**5. Payout Phase**
- Users claim their payouts
- Creators claim their fees
- Market becomes historical record

## The Four Settlement Criteria

All four must be met **simultaneously** for settlement to trigger:

### Criterion 1: Minimum Age

**Requirement:** Market must exist for at least the minimum duration (default: 7 days, set by creator)

**Why:** Gives debates time to develop. Prevents instant manipulation or premature settlement.

**What you see:**
```
✅ Age: 14 days (required: 7 days)
```

**Status:** Once met, stays met (time only goes forward)

### Criterion 2: Historical Stability

**Requirement:** Time-weighted price variance must be below 0.0001 (approximately 1% standard deviation)

**What this means:** Prices have been stable over the debate's history, weighted by how long each price persisted.

**Why time-weighted:** A 10-minute price spike contributes minimal weight. Days of stability dominate. This naturally filters brief manipulation.

**What you see:**
```
✅ Historical Stability: Low (variance: 0.00008 < 0.0001)
```

**This can flip:** If sudden volatility returns, criterion fails again until prices re-stabilize.

### Criterion 3: Current Agreement

**Requirement:** Current prices must be within 2% of their historical time-weighted averages

**What this means:** Recent prices match long-term prices. No last-second manipulation.

**Why:** Prevents "sniping"—someone dramatically moving prices right before settlement.

**What you see:**
```
✅ Current Agreement: Yes (max deviation: 1.2% < 2%)
```

**This can flip:** If prices suddenly move away from historical means, criterion fails.

### Criterion 4: Sustained Confirmation

**Requirement:** Criteria 2 and 3 must hold for 5+ consecutive checks (minimum 2.5 hours with 30-min update interval)

**What this means:** Stability isn't luck—it's sustained across multiple independent observations.

**Why:** Prevents settlement during temporary calm that might reverse.

**What you see:**
```
⏳ Confirmations: 3/5 (needs 2 more checks)
```

**This resets:** If criteria 2 or 3 fail at any check, counter resets to 0.

### How Checks Happen

**Automatic during trades:**
- When someone buys/sells ACTIVE opinions
- If 30+ minutes passed since last check
- System updates statistics and checks criteria

**Manual trigger:**
- Anyone can click "Update Settlement Status"
- If 30+ minutes passed since last check
- Costs gas (~$0.10), paid by whoever clicks

**30-minute cooldown:**
- Prevents spam updates
- Batches trades for efficiency
- Limits on-chain computation

## When Settlement Triggers

### The Moment

**When all 4 criteria met on a check:**

1. **Market freezes instantly**
   - No more trades accepted
   - No more staking/unstaking
   - No more opinion replacements

2. **Final state recorded**
   - Current prices become final prices
   - Current quantities frozen
   - Settlement block number recorded

3. **Finality countdown begins**
   - 100-block confirmation period
   - Prevents settlement during blockchain reorg
   - Safety measure for high-value outcomes

### What Happens to Prices

**Final prices are locked permanently:**

**Example:**
- Opinion A: 45%
- Opinion B: 38%
- Opinion C: 32%
- Opinion D: 25%

These become the **consensus distribution** for this debate.

### What About Candidates?

**Only ACTIVE opinions at settlement receive price-based payouts.**

**Candidate opinions:**
- Don't have prices (not in AMM)
- Remain in candidate pool
- Users can claim proportional stake
- No deadline to unstake

**If your opinion is candidate at settlement:**
- You can unstake your proportional share anytime
- No settlement payout based on price
- Your stake value determined by pool total

## The 100-Block Finality Period

### Why Wait?

**Blockchain reorgs** (chain reorganizations) can theoretically reverse recent blocks.

**100 blocks ensures:**
- Settlement is truly final
- Won't be undone by reorg
- Safe to distribute payouts

**Duration:** ~20 minutes on Polygon (2-second block times)

### During This Period

**You cannot:**
- Claim payouts yet
- Claim fees yet
- Trade (market is frozen)

**You can:**
- View final prices
- Calculate expected payouts
- Prepare claim transactions

**The waiting period:**
```
Settlement triggered at block 12,345,000
Finality reached at block 12,345,100
Time remaining: ~10 minutes
```

### After Finality

**Once 100 blocks pass:**
- Payouts become claimable
- Fees become claimable
- Market enters permanent archive state

## Claiming Your Payout

### Who Gets Paid

**You receive payout if:**
- You hold shares in an ACTIVE opinion at settlement
- Payout = (Your shares) × (Final price)

**Example:**
- You hold 150 shares in Opinion B
- Final price: 0.38
- Gross payout: 150 × 0.38 = $57.00
- Minus 1% fee (if enabled): $56.43

### How to Claim

**Navigate to the settled debate:**

1. You'll see "Claim Payout" button for each opinion you hold
2. Click to claim for one opinion
3. Or click "Claim All" to claim multiple opinions at once
4. Review transaction (gas ~$0.10-0.30)
5. Confirm in wallet
6. USDC sent to your wallet

### Multiple Opinions

**If you hold positions in multiple opinions:**

**Option A: Claim individually**
- One transaction per opinion
- More gas total but granular control

**Option B: Claim all at once**
- Single transaction
- More gas-efficient
- Saves time

**Recommended:** Claim all at once unless you have specific reasons not to.

### No Deadline

**Critical:** Payout claims have **NO expiration**.

You can claim:
- Immediately after finality period
- Days later
- Weeks later
- Years later

**Your payout is guaranteed forever.** The contract tracks your entitlement permanently.

**Why no deadline:** Your payout is your capital. You should never lose it due to missing a deadline.

### Fees Apply

**When you claim:**
- 1% total fee on payout amount (if all fees enabled)
- 0.5% platform fee (currently disabled by governance)
- 0.25% debate creator fee
- 0.25% opinion creator fee

**Current state (platform disabled):**
- 0.5% total fee
- 0.25% to debate creator
- 0.25% to opinion creator

**Example:**
- Payout: $100.00
- Fee (0.5%): $0.50
- You receive: $99.50

## What Happens to Candidates

### If Your Opinion Was Candidate

**At settlement, candidate opinions:**
- Remain in candidate pool
- Keep their accumulated stake
- Don't receive price-based payouts

**Your shares:**
- Still represent proportional claim on stake
- Can unstake anytime (no deadline)
- Receive your proportional USDC

**Example:**
- Candidate pool: $200 total
- You own 15% of shares
- You can claim: $30 (minus 1% fee if enabled)

### Unstaking After Settlement

**Process:**
1. Navigate to debate page
2. Find your candidate opinion
3. Click "Unstake"
4. Enter amount or "Max"
5. Confirm transaction
6. Receive USDC

**No deadline** - unstake whenever you want.

## Settlement Examples

### Example 1: Clean Settlement

**Week 1-2:** Active trading, prices volatile
**Week 3:** Prices stabilizing around equilibrium
**Week 4, Day 1:**
```
✅ Age: 22 days
✅ Stability: 0.00007
✅ Agreement: 1.1% deviation
⏳ Confirmations: 1/5
```

**Week 4, Days 2-3:** Confirmations accumulate
```
✅ Age: 23 days
✅ Stability: 0.00006
✅ Agreement: 0.9% deviation
✅ Confirmations: 5/5 → SETTLEMENT TRIGGERS
```

**20 minutes later:** Finality reached, payouts claimable

### Example 2: False Start

**Week 3:**
```
✅ Age: 18 days
✅ Stability: 0.00009
✅ Agreement: 1.5% deviation
⏳ Confirmations: 3/5
```

**Then:** Large trade creates volatility
```
✅ Age: 18 days
❌ Stability: 0.00015 (too high)
❌ Confirmations: 0/5 (reset)
```

**Outcome:** Settlement delayed, criteria must be rebuilt

### Example 3: Near-Settlement Exit

**Sarah sees settlement approaching:**
```
✅ 3/4 criteria met
⏳ Confirmations: 4/5
```

**Sarah's decision:**
- Current price: 42%
- She bought at 38%
- Up 10.5%

**Options:**
- **Wait for settlement:** Final price might be higher or lower than 42%
- **Exit now:** Lock in 10.5% gain, avoid uncertainty

**She exits:** Sells for $42 per share, pays 1% fee, done

**Next check:** Settlement triggers at 44% final price

**Result:** Sarah got 10.5% gain, would have been 15.8% if she held. But she avoided risk of final price being lower.

## After Settlement

### Market Becomes Historical Record

**The settled debate:**
- Shows final prices prominently
- Displays settlement date/block
- Preserves all history (trades, comments, replacements)
- Remains viewable forever
- Can be referenced in future debates

### Analytics and Learning

**Settled debates provide data:**
- Time to settlement (efficiency measure)
- Final distribution (consensus revealed)
- Trading patterns (behavior analysis)
- Replacement history (opinion evolution)

**This data helps:**
- Future debate creators
- Researchers studying consensus
- System improvements
- Parameter tuning

### Your Track Record

**Settled debates contribute to:**
- Your profitability record
- Your participation history
- Your reputation
- Your learning

**Profile shows:**
- Total debates participated in
- Total payouts claimed
- Overall P&L
- Success rate

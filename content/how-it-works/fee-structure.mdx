# Fee Structure

Trigger Market uses a simple, transparent fee system. Understanding it helps you know exactly what you're paying and earning.

## The Core Principle

**Fees charged only on exits.** Never on entries.

**Exits include:**
- Selling active shares
- Unstaking from candidates
- Claiming settlement payouts

**No fees on:**
- Buying active shares
- Staking on candidates
- Opinion replacements (gas only)
- Just holding positions

## Total Fee: 1%

When all fees are enabled (governance controlled):

**1% of exit value**, split three ways:

```
Exit Amount: $100.00
├─ Platform Fee: $0.50 (0.5%)
├─ Debate Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
└─ Opinion Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
──────────────────────────────
Total Fee: $1.00 (1.0%)
You Receive: $99.00
```

### Current State

**Platform fees: DISABLED** (0%)
**Creator fees: ENABLED** (0.5% total)

**Current split:**
```
Exit Amount: $100.00
├─ Platform Fee: $0.00 (disabled)
├─ Debate Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
└─ Opinion Creator Fee: $0.25 (0.25%)
──────────────────────────────
Total Fee: $0.50 (0.5%)
```

**Governance can toggle these independently.**

## Fee Breakdown

### Platform Fee (0.5%)

**Purpose:** Funds ongoing development, operations, infrastructure

**Current status:** Disabled (0%)

**Can be enabled by:** Governance vote with 7-day announcement before activation

**Goes to:** Platform treasury address

**Why disabled initially:** Reduces barriers to adoption, lets ecosystem grow

### Debate Creator Fee (0.25%)

**Purpose:** Rewards whoever created the debate

**Goes to:** The address that deployed the debate contract

**Earned on:** All trading activity in that debate

**Forever:** Accumulates as long as debate exists and trades happen

**Example:**
- You create a debate
- It generates $50K in exit volume over lifetime
- You earn: $50K × 0.25% = $125 (if creator fees enabled)

### Opinion Creator Fee (0.25%)

**Purpose:** Rewards whoever proposed the opinion

**Goes to:** The address that originally proposed that specific opinion

**Earned on:** All trading involving that specific opinion

**Tracked via:** `opinionOriginalCreator[opinionId]` (immutable)

**Example:**
- You propose Opinion X
- It generates $30K in exit volume
- You earn: $30K × 0.25% = $75 (if creator fees enabled)

**For initial opinions:** The debate creator is also the opinion creator, so they get both fees (0.5% total on those opinions)

## Fee Accumulation (Pull Pattern)

### How It Works

**Fees don't get sent immediately.** They accumulate in the contract.

**Flow:**
```
Trade/Exit Happens
    ↓
Fee Calculated
    ↓
Added to claimableFees[creatorAddress]
    ↓
Creator Claims When Ready
    ↓
USDC Sent to Creator
```

### Why Pull Pattern?

**Benefits:**

1. **Gas efficient:** One transfer when claiming vs. three transfers per trade
2. **Resilient:** Blocklisted addresses can't break trading
3. **Batching:** Claim fees from multiple debates at once
4. **Flexibility:** Claim when you want, not forced

**Tracking:**
```solidity
mapping(address => uint256) claimableFees
```

Each address can see their accumulated fees onchain anytime.

## Critical Deadlines

### Fee Claims: 365-Day Deadline

<Callout title="Important: Fee Claim Deadline" type="warn">
**Creator fees must be claimed within 365 days of debate settlement.**

After 365 days, unclaimed fees remain in the contract permanently and cannot be withdrawn.
</Callout>

**Timeline:**
```
Day 0: Debate settles
Day 0-100 blocks: Finality period
Day 0-365: Fee claim window OPEN
Day 365: Fee claim window CLOSES
Day 366+: Unclaimed fees locked forever
```

**Example:**
- Debate settles January 1, 2025
- You earned $200 in creator fees
- **You have until January 1, 2026** to claim
- If you don't claim by then, the $200 is locked forever

**Who this affects:**
- Debate creators
- Opinion creators
- Platform (if fees enabled)

**Why 365 days:** Balances giving creators time vs. not leaving funds in limbo indefinitely.

### Payout Claims: NO Deadline

<Callout title="Good News: Payouts Never Expire" type="info">
**Settlement payouts (your shares × final price) have NO deadline.**

You can claim your payouts anytime after the 100-block finality period. No rush. No expiration.
</Callout>

**Why the difference?**

**Fees** = Creator earnings (incentive to stay engaged)
**Payouts** = User's own capital (should never be lost)

**Example:**
- Debate settles January 2025
- Your payout: $500
- You can claim in 2025, 2026, 2030, whenever
- Your $500 is safe forever

## Claiming Your Fees

### As a Creator

**When you have accumulated fees:**

1. Navigate to Profile → Earnings
2. See all claimable fees
3. Options:
   - Claim from one debate (one transaction)
   - Claim from multiple debates (batch transaction)
   - Claim all at once (most gas-efficient)

4. Click "Claim Fees"
5. Review transaction
6. Confirm in wallet
7. USDC sent to your address

**Gas cost:** ~$0.10-0.30 depending on number of debates

### Timing Strategy

**Claim frequently:**
- Less risk of missing 365-day deadline
- More transactions, more total gas

**Claim infrequently (batch):**
- Fewer transactions, less total gas
- More efficient
- But don't wait until day 364

**Recommended:** Claim every few months, well before deadlines.

### Missing the Deadline

**If you miss the 365-day window:**
- Fees are locked in contract forever
- No recovery mechanism
- No exceptions

**To avoid:**
- Set calendar reminders
- Claim regularly
- Don't procrastinate

**The system will warn you:**
- Profile shows countdown
- Notifications as deadline approaches
- Email alerts (if enabled)

## Governance Control

### Fee Toggles

Two independent switches:

**Switch 1: Platform Fees**
- Currently: OFF (0%)
- Can be turned ON by governance
- Requires 7-day public announcement before activation
- Once ON, platform receives 0.5% of all exits

**Switch 2: Creator Fees**
- Currently: ON (0.5% total)
- Can be turned OFF by governance
- If OFF, only platform fees apply (if enabled)
- Debate creators: 0.25%, Opinion creators: 0.25%

### Possible States

| Platform | Creator | Total Fee | Distribution |
|----------|---------|-----------|--------------|
| OFF | OFF | 0% | No fees |
| OFF | ON | 0.5% | Creators only (current) |
| ON | OFF | 0.5% | Platform only |
| ON | ON | 1.0% | All three parties |

### Why Governance Control?

**Flexibility:**
- Start with low fees (adoption)
- Adjust based on ecosystem needs
- Balance sustainability vs. growth

**Transparency:**
- All changes require governance votes
- Community decides fee structure
- No unilateral changes

**Alignment:**
- Fees serve ecosystem health
- Adjusted based on evidence
- Responsive to community feedback

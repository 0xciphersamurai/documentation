# Ways to Participate

There are several ways to participate in Trigger Market beyond just trading. This section covers all the ways you can engage with the platform.

## What You'll Learn

- **Trading Opinions** — The mechanics of buying and selling shares in opinions
- **Creating Debates** — How to launch your own consensus markets and earn creator fees
- **Proposing Opinions** — Adding new perspectives to existing debates
- **Why Participation Matters** — The broader impact of consensus markets

## Ways to Earn

Trigger Market offers multiple ways to earn:

1. **Trading** — Buy low, sell high, or hold until settlement
2. **Creating Debates** — Earn 0.25% of all exit volume in your debates
3. **Proposing Opinions** — Earn 0.25% when people trade your opinions

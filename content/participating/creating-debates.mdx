# Creating Debates

Anyone can create a consensus market. When you do, you earn a share of all trading fees forever. Here's how to launch quality debates.

## Why Create Debates?

### Economic Incentive

**You earn 0.25% of all trading volume** in your debate. Forever.

**Example:**
- You create a debate
- Over its lifetime, it generates $100,000 in trading volume
- You earn: $100,000 × 0.25% = $250

Popular debates can generate significant fees over weeks or months.

### Intellectual Contribution

You're:
- Framing important questions
- Creating spaces for collective intelligence
- Contributing to coordination infrastructure
- Building reputation as a thought leader

### Community Service

Good debates help communities:
- Make better decisions
- Discover consensus
- Coordinate effectively
- Resolve important questions

## What Makes a Good Debate?

### 1. Important Question

**Good:**
- "Which governance model should our DAO adopt?"
- "Best technical approach for scaling solution?"
- "What principles should guide our community?"

**Bad:**
- "Will it rain tomorrow?" (objective, has oracle)
- "What's 2+2?" (not debatable)
- "Which color is best?" (too arbitrary, no stakes)

**Test:** Would a community genuinely benefit from discovering consensus on this question?

### 2. Multiple Viable Perspectives

The debate needs legitimate alternatives:

**Good:**
- 4-6 distinct, well-formulated opinions
- Each has merit
- Reasonable people could support any
- No obviously "correct" answer

**Bad:**
- Only 2 options (too binary)
- One option clearly superior (no real debate)
- Options not mutually exclusive
- Poorly defined alternatives

### 3. Engaged Audience

**Ask:**
- Who cares about this question?
- Will they participate?
- Is there enough interest to generate activity?
- Can you reach this audience?

Without an audience, even great debates fail.

### 4. Appropriate Timing

**Consider:**
- Is this question ripe for resolution?
- Or too early (not enough information)?
- Or too late (consensus already formed)?

Timing matters.

## The Creation Process

### Step 1: Frame the Question

**Your debate title** should be:
- Clear and specific
- Not too broad ("Best programming language" is too broad)
- Not too narrow ("Best way to format this specific line of code" is too narrow)
- Neutral (not leading toward a conclusion)

**Good examples:**
- "Most promising approach to [specific problem]"
- "Best strategy for [specific situation]"
- "How should [community] handle [decision]?"

**Maximum 500 characters** for title.

### Step 2: Write the Description

**Include:**
- Context: What's being decided and why it matters
- Stakes: Why this question is important
- Scope: What's in/out of bounds
- Timeline: Any relevant deadlines or milestones

**Aim for:**
- 500-1500 words
- Clear and accessible
- Neutral tone
- Markdown formatting supported

**Maximum 10,000 characters**.

### Step 3: Define Initial Opinions

You must provide **3-10 initial opinions**.

**For each opinion:**

**Title** (required):
- Clear, concise label
- What this perspective is called
- Maximum 200 characters

**Description** (required):
- Full argument for this perspective
- Why it has merit
- Supporting reasoning
- Evidence if available
- Maximum 10,000 characters each

**Tips:**
- Represent each opinion fairly
- Don't strawman weak options
- Give each a fighting chance
- Include diverse perspectives

**The 10-opinion hard cap:**
- Ensures cognitive clarity
- Maintains UX simplicity
- Preserves mathematical stability
- Forces curation of best options

### Step 4: Set Initial Liquidity

**Minimum: $50 USDC** (governance parameter, may adjust)

**This amount:**
- Distributed equally across all initial opinions
- Seeds the AMM
- You receive proportional shares
- Can't be withdrawn (becomes market liquidity)

**How to choose:**
- More liquidity = more stable initial prices
- Less liquidity = more volatile, higher risk
- Consider your budget and expected activity

**Example with 4 opinions:**
- You deposit $80
- Each opinion gets $20 worth of initial quantity
- All start at equal prices
- You hold shares worth $80

### Step 5: Set Settlement Parameters (Optional)

**Minimum debate duration:**
- Default: 7 days
- You can set higher (e.g., 30 days for critical decisions)
- Cannot set lower than 1 day
- Cannot exceed 365 days

**Why customize:**
- Important decisions need more time
- Casual debates can settle faster
- Depends on expected pace of discussion

Other parameters (variance thresholds, etc.) use system defaults.

### Step 6: Review and Deploy

**Preview shows:**
- How debate will appear
- Initial opinion layout
- Gas cost estimate (~$0.50-2.00 depending on opinion count)
- Total cost (gas + liquidity)

**Confirmation modal:**
- Final check before deploying
- Smart contract details
- Transaction breakdown

**Click "Create Debate":**
- Sign transaction in wallet
- Wait for confirmation (~2 seconds on Base)
- Debate deploys with unique address
- You're redirected to the debate page

**Done!** Your debate is live.

## After Creation

### Promote Your Debate

**Share widely:**
- Social media (Twitter, Discord, Telegram)
- Relevant communities and forums
- Direct outreach to stakeholders
- Blog posts or articles explaining context

**Without promotion:** Even great debates languish unused.

### Seed Initial Trading

**Consider:**
- Making some trades yourself (express your view)
- Getting early supporters to participate
- Creating initial price discovery
- Demonstrating the mechanics

Active markets attract more participation.

### Engage in Discussion

**Use the comments:**
- Provide context and updates
- Answer questions
- Moderate constructively
- Keep discussion healthy

You're the debate "host" in some sense.

### Monitor Health

**Watch for:**
- Trading activity (or lack thereof)
- Quality of discussion
- Spam or manipulation attempts
- Settlement progress

### Update Community

**Share milestones:**
- "100 traders participated!"
- "Major opinion replacement today"
- "Approaching settlement criteria"
- "Consensus emerging around Option X"

Keep people engaged.

## Earning Fees

### How Fees Accumulate

Every trade in your debate:
- Generates 1% fee on exits (when all fees enabled)
- You receive 0.25% of that
- Accumulates in smart contract
- Tracked per your address

**Example:**
- User sells $100 worth
- Total fee: $1.00
- Your share: $0.25
- Accumulates in `claimableFees[yourAddress]`

### Claiming Fees

**After debate settles:**
1. Navigate to your profile
2. Go to "Earnings" tab
3. See claimable fees per debate
4. Click "Claim Fees"
5. Transaction sends USDC to your wallet

**Important: 365-day deadline** to claim fees after settlement. After that, unclaimed fees are locked forever.

### Maximizing Fee Income

**Create popular debates:**
- Choose important topics
- Frame well
- Promote effectively
- Foster healthy discussion

**Volume is key:**
- $10K volume at 0.25% = $25
- $100K volume at 0.25% = $250
- $1M volume at 0.25% = $2,500

Active, engaging debates generate more fees.

## Quality Standards

### What the Community Values

**Well-researched:**
- Opinions thoroughly described
- Context provided
- Fair representation

**Appropriate scope:**
- Not too broad
- Not too narrow
- Clear boundaries

**Neutral framing:**
- No bias in description
- All opinions given fair chance
- Let the market decide

**Clean execution:**
- No errors in opinion text
- Proper formatting
- Professional presentation

### What to Avoid

**Spam debates:**
- Low-effort, copy-paste
- Trivial questions
- Just trying to earn fees

**Biased framing:**
- One option obviously favored
- Unfair descriptions
- Manipulation attempts

**Duplicate debates:**
- Same question already active
- Fragments activity
- Confuses users

**Abandoned debates:**
- Creator disappears
- No moderation
- No community building

Quality matters. The community remembers.

## Advanced Strategies

### Multi-Staged Debates

**Approach:**
- Create follow-up debates based on results
- "We chose X, now how should we implement it?"
- Build narrative across debates

**Benefit:**
- Sustained engagement
- Deeper exploration
- Multiple fee opportunities

### Collaborative Creation

**Approach:**
- Work with community to design debate
- Crowdsource opinions
- Collective ownership

**Benefit:**
- Built-in audience
- Better design
- Shared promotion

### Debate Series

**Approach:**
- Create related debates on a theme
- "Best X for Y" across different Ys
- Build expertise in a domain

**Benefit:**
- Reputation as curator
- Efficient reuse of research
- Community expectations

### Timely Debates

**Approach:**
- Create debates about current hot topics
- Move quickly when issues emerge
- Capture attention

**Benefit:**
- High initial activity
- Relevance boost
- Faster fee accumulation

## Legal and Ethical Considerations

### Neutrality

Present all opinions fairly, even ones you disagree with. You're creating infrastructure, not pushing an agenda.

### Intellectual Property

Don't use copyrighted material without permission. Write original descriptions or properly attribute.

### Sensitive Topics

Some debates may involve controversial subjects. Consider:
- Potential for harm
- Community standards
- Your own comfort level

You have freedom, but also responsibility.

### Disclosure

If you have conflicts of interest (financial stake in an outcome, relationships with opinion proponents), consider disclosing in the debate description.

## The Creator's Role

As a debate creator, you're:

**A facilitator** - Creating space for discovery
**A curator** - Selecting which perspectives to include
**A moderator** - Fostering healthy discussion
**An entrepreneur** - Building something valuable

But you're not:

**A judge** - The market decides, not you
**A manipulator** - Let consensus emerge naturally
**A dictator** - Participants shape the debate

Your role is to enable, not control.

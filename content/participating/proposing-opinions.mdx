# Proposing Opinions

Sometimes the best perspective isn't in the original debate. When you spot what's missing and propose it, you can earn fees if it gains traction.

## Why Propose Opinions?

### Fill the Gap

Initial debates often miss important perspectives:
- Creator couldn't anticipate every angle
- New information emerged
- Framings proved inadequate
- Better alternatives exist

**You can add what's missing.**

### Earn Creator Fees

**You earn 0.25% of all trading volume** for your opinion. Forever.

When your opinion:
- Enters the AMM (gets traded)
- Stays active
- Generates volume

**You earn fees on every trade** involving that opinion.

**Example:**
- Your opinion generates $50K in trading volume
- You earn: $50K × 0.25% = $125

Popular opinions earn significant fees.

### Shape the Debate

By proposing quality opinions, you:
- Improve debate quality
- Expand the conversation
- Contribute better perspectives
- Help consensus formation

## What Makes a Good Opinion?

### 1. Genuinely Missing Perspective

**Good proposals:**
- Offer something current options don't
- Address gaps in the debate
- Represent legitimate alternative viewpoints
- Would actually help the discussion

**Bad proposals:**
- Duplicate existing options
- Offer nothing new
- Just trying to earn fees
- Low-effort spam

**Test:** Would the debate be better with this opinion included?

### 2. Well-Articulated

**Your opinion should have:**
- Clear thesis statement
- Supporting arguments
- Evidence or reasoning
- Acknowledgment of trade-offs
- Professional presentation

**Quality matters.** The community won't stake on poorly written opinions.

### 3. Appropriately Timed

**Consider:**
- Is the debate still active?
- Has consensus already formed?
- Is there room for this perspective?
- Will it get attention?

Proposing to a near-settled debate wastes effort.

### 4. Viable Competitor

**Ask honestly:**
- Could this actually win consensus?
- Or is it clearly inferior to existing options?
- Does it have realistic support potential?

Don't propose doomed opinions just to claim creator fees.

## The Proposal Process

### Step 1: Find the Right Debate

**Look for debates where:**
- Active discussion happening
- Current opinions inadequate
- Gap you can fill
- Community would appreciate the addition

**Browse:**
- Trending debates (most activity)
- Topics you understand deeply
- Areas where you have expertise

### Step 2: Formulate Your Opinion

**Title:**
- Clear, concise label
- What this perspective is called
- Maximum 200 characters
- Example: "Hybrid Approach: Combine A and B"

**Description:**
- Full argument for this perspective
- Why it has merit
- How it differs from existing options
- Supporting evidence
- Maximum 10,000 characters

**Use Markdown:**
- Headers, lists, emphasis
- Make it readable
- Structure your argument

### Step 3: Set Initial Stake

**You must stake a minimum amount** (typically $10-25, governance parameter).

This demonstrates:
- You believe in this opinion
- You have skin in the game
- This isn't spam

**How much to stake:**
- Enough to show seriousness
- Check replacement threshold (need 105% of weakest opinion's vault value to enter)
- Consider if you want to get close to threshold alone or rely on others

**Your stake:**
- Goes into candidate pool
- Others can add to it
- You own proportional shares
- Can unstake if opinion doesn't gain traction

### Step 4: Submit Proposal

**Review preview:**
- How it will appear
- Your initial stake
- Gas cost (~$0.10-0.30)

**Confirm transaction:**
- Sign in wallet
- Wait for confirmation
- Opinion appears in "Candidate Opinions" tab

**Done!** Your opinion is now a candidate.

## After Proposing

### Promote Your Opinion

**Share why it matters:**
- Comment in the debate explaining your thinking
- Share on social media
- Direct outreach to stakeholders
- Present the case

**Without promotion:** Good opinions can languish unnoticed.

### Engage with Feedback

**In the comments:**
- Answer questions
- Respond to critiques
- Refine your arguments
- Build support

Engagement helps build momentum.

### Monitor Progress

**Watch the progress bar:**
```
$45 / $105 needed (105% of weakest active)
43% to replacement threshold
```

**Track:**
- Who's staking on your opinion
- How fast stake is accumulating
- What the weakest active opinion's value is (threshold changes)

### Add More Stake (Optional)

**If your opinion is close but needs a push:**
- Add more of your own stake
- Shows continued conviction
- Helps reach threshold

**But don't force it:** If organic support isn't coming, maybe the opinion isn't resonating.

## Getting Into the AMM

### Reaching the Threshold

Your opinion can replace the weakest when:

**Your stake ≥ 1.05 × (Weakest opinion's vault value)**

**Example:**
- Weakest opinion: vault value $100
- Your opinion needs: $105+
- The 5% buffer prevents immediate flip-flops

### Triggering Replacement

**Once eligible:**
- Anyone can click "Replace Weakest Opinion"
- Gas cost: ~$0.10-0.30 (paid by trigger)
- Replacement happens automatically
- Contract identifies weakest and swaps

**You don't need to trigger it yourself** - anyone can (and will, since it costs little).

### What Happens

**Your opinion:**
- Exits candidate pool
- Enters AMM with calculated quantity
- Now has a price
- Can be traded

**Replaced opinion:**
- Exits AMM
- Becomes candidate with its liquidation value
- Can potentially return later

**Your stake:**
- Converts to AMM shares proportionally
- You now hold AMM shares
- Can trade them or hold

**Example:**
- You staked $30 out of $110 total (27% ownership)
- Opinion enters with quantity q_new = 25 units
- You receive 27% of 25 = 6.75 units
- Current price: 0.38
- Your position value: 6.75 × 0.38 = $2.565

### Earning Fees

**From this point forward:**
- Every trade involving your opinion generates fees
- You earn 0.25% of the volume
- Accumulates in the contract
- Claimable after settlement (365-day deadline)

## If Your Opinion Stays Candidate

### Managing Disappointment

**Not every proposal succeeds.** That's okay.

**Possible reasons:**
- Timing wasn't right
- Similar to existing option
- Didn't resonate with community
- Communication could have been better

### Options

**1. Unstake and Exit:**
- Get your initial stake back (minus 1% fee if enabled)
- Move on to other opportunities
- Try again elsewhere

**2. Hold and Wait:**
- Maybe support will come later
- New information might help
- Patience could pay off
- No deadline to unstake

**3. Adjust and Repromote:**
- Refine your arguments in comments
- Address critiques
- Try different messaging
- See if fresh promotion helps

### Learning

**What can you learn?**
- Was the opinion actually valuable?
- Could you have presented it better?
- Was timing the issue?
- What would you do differently?

Each attempt teaches something.

## After Entering the AMM

### Your Opinion is Now Active

**It can:**
- Be traded (generates fees for you)
- Rise or fall in price
- Eventually get replaced by another candidate
- Be present at settlement

### Managing Your Position

**You hold shares as the proposer.**

**Decisions:**
- Hold through settlement (conviction play)
- Sell if price rises (take profits)
- Add more if you want more exposure
- Manage like any other position

**Your creator fees are separate from your position** - you earn 0.25% regardless of your holdings.

### If Your Opinion Gets Replaced

**Your opinion exits the AMM:**
- Becomes candidate again
- Receives liquidation value
- Your shares convert to candidate stake claims

**Your creator fees continue:**
- If it re-enters later, fees resume
- Previous trading already generated fees (claimable after settlement)

**Can cycle multiple times:**
- CANDIDATE → ACTIVE → CANDIDATE → ACTIVE...
- You earn fees whenever it's active and trading

## Multiple Proposals

**You can propose multiple opinions:**
- In the same debate (if you see multiple gaps)
- Across different debates
- Building portfolio of perspectives

**Each opinion:**
- Separate candidate pool
- Separate stake requirement
- Separate fee earning potential

**Strategy:**
- Diversify across debates/topics
- One "hit" opinion can earn substantial fees
- Quality over quantity

## Best Practices

### Do Your Research

**Before proposing:**
- Read all existing opinions thoroughly
- Understand the debate context
- Check if your idea is already covered
- Evaluate if there's genuine demand

### Write Quality Content

**Your description is your pitch:**
- Clear, structured, convincing
- Acknowledge counterarguments
- Provide evidence
- Professional presentation

### Be Patient

**Good opinions take time:**
- Building awareness
- Accumulating support
- Entering AMM

Don't expect instant success.

### Engage Constructively

**In the debate:**
- Discuss ideas, not personalities
- Accept critique gracefully
- Refine arguments based on feedback
- Build relationships

### Respect the Process

**Don't:**
- Spam multiple low-quality proposals
- Manipulate or shill aggressively
- Create sock puppets to stake
- Game the system

Reputation matters.

## The Economics

### Fee Potential

**If your opinion:**
- Enters AMM
- Stays active through settlement
- Generates $100K volume

**You earn: $250** (0.25% of $100K)

**If it cycles:**
- Active for 2 weeks, generates $30K → You earn $75
- Replaced, then re-enters
- Active for 3 more weeks, generates $50K → You earn $125
- Total: $200

**Fees accumulate** across all active periods.

### Risk/Reward

**Cost:** Initial stake ($10-25) + gas (~$0.30)
**Potential:** Unlimited fee earnings if opinion succeeds
**Risk:** Lose initial stake if opinion fails badly

**Break-even:** Need enough volume to cover initial stake + opportunity cost.

**Example:**
- Stake $20
- Need $20 / 0.25% = $8,000 in volume to break even
- Popular opinions easily exceed this

## Advanced Strategies

### Strategic Timing

**Propose when:**
- Debate has momentum
- Clear gap exists
- You can get initial supporters lined up
- Market is receptive

### Coalition Building

**Before proposing:**
- Discuss with community
- Line up early stakers
- Create shared ownership
- Built-in promotion network

### Iterative Refinement

**Use comments to:**
- Test reception before formal proposal
- Refine based on feedback
- Build consensus around framing
- Launch with momentum

### Portfolio Approach

**Propose multiple opinions across debates:**
- Diversify risk
- Learn what works
- Build reputation
- Increase odds of success

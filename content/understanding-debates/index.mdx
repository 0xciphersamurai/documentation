# Understanding Debates

This section explains the core mechanics of debates on Trigger Market—how opinions work, how prices form, and how consensus emerges.

## What You'll Learn

- **Opinions and Prices** — What prices represent and how they reflect collective belief
- **The Opinion Lifecycle** — How opinions move between active and candidate states
- **How Consensus Forms** — The settlement criteria and what price stability means
- **Dynamic Evolution** — How markets adapt as better framings emerge

## Key Concepts

A debate is a market where multiple opinions compete for support. Each opinion has:

- **A price** — reflecting its current level of support
- **Shareholders** — people who have bought into that opinion
- **A lifecycle state** — either active (tradeable) or candidate (waiting to enter)

Unlike prediction markets, debates don't have "correct" answers. The final price distribution *is* the answer—showing what participants with skin in the game collectively believe.

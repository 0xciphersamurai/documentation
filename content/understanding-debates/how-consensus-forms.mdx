# How Consensus Forms

The breakthrough of consensus markets is **settlement without oracles**. But how does the system actually know when consensus is reached? The answer: by watching the market itself.

## The Core Insight

When people with real money at stake stop changing their positions, consensus has formed.

Think of it like a pendulum:
- **Early**: Wild swings as opinions clash
- **Middle**: Dampening oscillations as patterns emerge
- **Late**: Tiny fluctuations around equilibrium
- **Settled**: Still. The market has decided.

The system doesn't decide truth. It observes: **Have prices stabilized?**

## What Price Stability Means

### Active Debate = Moving Prices

In an active debate:
- New information arrives → people trade → prices change
- Arguments are made → opinions shift → prices adjust
- People reconsider → capital reallocates → prices move

High price variance means: **No consensus yet. Still debating.**

### Emerging Consensus = Slowing Movement

As debates mature:
- Most arguments have been made
- Strong positions reflected in prices
- Fewer trades, smaller price impacts
- Prices oscillate in narrower ranges

Decreasing volatility means: **Consensus forming. Getting closer.**

### Consensus Reached = Stable Prices

When consensus forms:
- Prices stopped moving meaningfully
- Participants expressed their views fully
- No new information changing minds
- Market at equilibrium

Stable prices over time means: **Consensus achieved. Ready to settle.**

## The Four Settlement Criteria

For a market to settle, **all four criteria** must be met simultaneously:

### 1. Minimum Age

**Requirement:** Market must exist for at least 7 days (default, set by creator)

**Why:** Prevents premature settlement. Gives debates time to develop, arguments to be made, information to flow.

**Example:** A market created Tuesday cannot settle before next Tuesday, regardless of price stability.

### 2. Historical Stability (Low Variance)

**Requirement:** Time-weighted price variance < 0.0001 (about 1% standard deviation)

**What this means:** Prices have been stable over the market's history, weighted by how long each price persisted.

**Why time-weighted:** A 10-minute price spike contributes negligible weight. Days of stability dominate the calculation. This naturally filters manipulation.

**Example:**
- Week 1: Prices volatile (variance: 0.05) ❌
- Week 2: Prices calmer (variance: 0.002) ❌
- Week 3: Prices stable (variance: 0.00008) ✅

### 3. Current Agreement (Near Historical Mean)

**Requirement:** Current prices must be within 2% of their historical averages

**What this means:** Recent prices match long-term prices. No recent manipulation shifting things.

**Why:** Prevents "sniping" where someone moves prices dramatically right before settlement.

**Example:**
- Historical average for Opinion A: 45%
- Current price: 44.5%
- Difference: 0.5% < 2% ✅

- If current price: 48%
- Difference: 3% > 2% ❌

### 4. Sustained Confirmation

**Requirement:** Conditions 2 and 3 must hold for 5+ consecutive checks (at least 2.5 hours with 30-min update intervals)

**What this means:** Stability isn't a lucky moment—it's sustained over multiple independent observations.

**Why:** Guards against temporary calm followed by renewed volatility.

**Example:**
```
Check 1 (12:00): Stable ✅ (1/5)
Check 2 (12:30): Stable ✅ (2/5)
Check 3 (13:00): Volatile ❌ (reset to 0/5)
Check 4 (13:30): Stable ✅ (1/5)
Check 5 (14:00): Stable ✅ (2/5)
Check 6 (14:30): Stable ✅ (3/5)
Check 7 (15:00): Stable ✅ (4/5)
Check 8 (15:30): Stable ✅ (5/5) → Settlement eligible
```

## How Tracking Works

### EWMA (Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages)

The system tracks price stability using EWMA—a mathematical technique that:

- **Weights recent prices more** than old prices (exponential decay)
- **Weights by duration** prices persisted, not just occurrence
- **Updates efficiently** without storing full price history
- **Detects stability** through variance calculation

You don't need to understand the math. The key property: **Brief manipulation filtered out, sustained stability detected.**

### Update Timing

Settlement criteria are checked:

1. **During trades:** If 30+ minutes passed since last check, automatically update
2. **Manual trigger:** Anyone can click "Update Settlement Status" (if 30+ min passed)

**30-minute cooldown:** Batches multiple trades into single update, saves gas, prevents spam.

### What You See

On the debate page:

```
Settlement Progress:
✅ Minimum Age: 14 days (required: 7 days)
✅ Historical Stability: Low (variance: 0.00008 < 0.0001)
✅ Current Agreement: Yes (max deviation: 1.2% < 2%)
⏳ Confirmations: 3/5 (needs 2 more consecutive checks)
```

When all show green checkmarks → settlement triggers automatically on next check.

## The Settlement Process

### When Criteria Met

1. **Market freezes immediately**
   - No more trading
   - No more staking/unstaking
   - No more replacements

2. **Final prices recorded**
   - Current prices become permanent
   - These determine all payouts

3. **100-block confirmation period begins**
   - Safety buffer (~20 minutes on Polygon)
   - Prevents settlement during potential blockchain reorganization

4. **After 100 blocks:**
   - Payouts become claimable
   - Fees become claimable (365-day deadline)

### Why 100 Blocks?

Blockchain finality isn't instant. A "reorg" (chain reorganization) could theoretically reverse recent blocks.

100 blocks ensures settlement is truly final and won't be undone. Standard safety practice for important state transitions.

## Why This Works

### Defense in Depth

Each criterion defends against different attacks:

**Minimum age** → Prevents instant manipulation at market creation
**Historical stability** → Ensures genuine consensus, not temporary calm
**Current agreement** → Blocks last-second sniping attempts
**Sustained confirmation** → Requires consistency over time, not luck

All four together create robust protection.

### Natural Manipulation Resistance

The time-weighting is key:

**Attacker's attempt:**
- Spend $10K to manipulate prices for 30 minutes
- Hope to trigger settlement

**What happens:**
- Those 30 minutes contribute minimal weight (30 min out of days/weeks)
- Historical variance barely changes
- Criteria not met
- Money spent, attack failed

**To actually trigger settlement through manipulation:**
- Must maintain artificial prices for *days*
- At massive cost (fighting against natural equilibrium)
- Economically irrational

The math naturally filters attacks without complex detection logic.

### Adaptive to Market Types

Different debates have different dynamics:

**Fast-moving debates:**
- High trading activity
- Frequent price changes
- More updates, more data
- System adapts, still detects stability when it comes

**Slow-moving debates:**
- Occasional trades
- Long periods of stability
- Fewer updates but still tracked correctly
- System works the same

The EWMA approach handles both gracefully.

## Practical Example

Let's follow a debate from creation to settlement:

### Week 1: Active Debate
```
Prices volatile: 52% → 48% → 55% → 50%
Variance: 0.02 (high)
Settlement status: ❌ Age met, ❌ Stability not met
```

### Week 2: Maturing
```
Prices calming: 53% → 52% → 53% → 52%
Variance: 0.005 (medium)
Settlement status: ✅ Age met, ❌ Stability not met
```

### Week 3: Converging
```
Prices stable: 52.5% → 52.3% → 52.6% → 52.4%
Variance: 0.00008 (low)
Current prices match historical average
Settlement status: ✅✅✅ Three criteria met, ⏳ Confirmations: 2/5
```

### Week 3, Days Later: Ready
```
Prices still stable for 5 consecutive checks
Settlement status: ✅✅✅✅ All criteria met
Next update: Settlement triggers
```

## Understanding Payouts

### Payout Calculation

**For each opinion you hold:**

Payout = (Your shares) × (Final price)

**Example:**
- You hold 200 shares in Opinion A
- Final price at settlement: 0.48
- Your gross payout: 200 × 0.48 = $96
- Minus 1% fee (if enabled): $95.04

### Only Active Opinions

**Important:** Only ACTIVE opinions (those in the AMM at settlement) receive payouts based on final prices.

If your opinion is CANDIDATE at settlement:
- No price (candidates don't have prices)
- Your claim is on the candidate stake pool
- You can unstake your proportional share (no deadline)

### Claiming

**No deadline for payouts:** Claim anytime after 100-block finality period.

The system tracks your entitled payout forever. No rush. No expiration.

(Fee claims for creators have 365-day deadline, but participant payouts never expire.)

<Callout title="Key Takeaway" type="info">
Settlement happens when observable price stability indicates consensus formation. No oracle judges truth. No authority declares winners. The market reveals its equilibrium, and that equilibrium determines outcomes.
</Callout>

## Why This Matters

Traditional prediction markets need:
- External events to happen
- Oracles to verify what happened
- Clear "right" answers

Consensus markets need:
- Participants to reach equilibrium
- Observable price stability
- Math to detect convergence

This unlocks an entirely new class of markets—for debates, not predictions. For coordination, not forecasting.

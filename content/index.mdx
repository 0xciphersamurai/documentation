# Welcome to Trigger Market

**Trigger Market** enables something that hasn't existed before: prediction markets that don't need oracles. Markets where the answer emerges from the participants themselves.

## What This Means

Traditional prediction markets work great for objective questions: "Who will win the election?" or "Will Bitcoin hit $100K?" These have clear, verifiable answers.

But what about questions that don't have objective answers? Questions where reasonable people disagree, and the answer is what the community collectively decides?

- "Which technical approach should we prioritize?"
- "What values should guide our DAO?"
- "Which research direction deserves funding?"

**These questions can now have markets too.** Not through some authority declaring truth, but through observable consensus formation.

## How It Works (In Brief)

1. **Someone creates a debate** with initial opinions
2. **People trade on opinions they believe in** - prices adjust based on support
3. **New opinions can challenge existing ones** - the market evolves dynamically
4. **When prices stabilize, consensus is reached** - no oracle needed
5. **Settlement happens automatically** based on final prices

The magic is in step 4: When people with real money at stake stop changing their positions, that *is* the consensus. The market has decided.

## Why This Matters

This unlocks an entirely new class of coordination tools:

- **DAOs** can gauge sentiment on governance decisions
- **Research communities** can prioritize competing approaches
- **Organizations** can discover collective preferences
- **Communities** can resolve debates with economic commitment

All without needing trusted authorities to declare outcomes.

## What Makes It Possible

Two key innovations work together:

**1. Mathematical Convergence**
The L² norm AMM (the math powering the market) has a beautiful property: as liquidity grows, prices naturally become more stable. This creates favorable conditions for consensus to emerge. You don't need to understand the math—just know it makes oracle-free settlement possible.

**2. Dynamic Evolution**
Unlike traditional markets where outcomes are fixed, opinions can compete for inclusion. Weak perspectives get replaced by stronger ones. The debate evolves as understanding deepens.

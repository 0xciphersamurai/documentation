# Welcome to Trigger Market

**Trigger Market** enables something that hasn't existed before: markets for subjective debates. Every online argument—from "best anime of 2025" to "pineapple on pizza"—can have stakes. No oracles. No gatekeepers. Just skin in the game.

## The Problem

People argue online constantly with zero consequences:

- Reddit threads about anything and everything
- Twitter fights that rage forever
- Discord debates with no resolution
- YouTube comments full of claims
- Forum wars that never end

**None of it matters because nobody has skin in the game.**

You can be wrong a thousand times. You can take extreme positions for engagement. You can virtue signal without cost. Arguments are free, so they mean nothing.

## The Solution

**Turn any debate into a market where people back their opinions with capital.**

When you have to put money on your take, things get real:
- False conviction gets exposed
- Keyboard warriors revealed
- Virtue signaling becomes expensive
- Being right becomes profitable
- Truth-seeking has incentives

## How It Works (In Brief)

1. **Someone creates a debate** - any question people argue about
2. **People trade on opinions they believe in** - prices adjust based on support
3. **New opinions can challenge existing ones** - the market evolves dynamically
4. **When prices stabilize, consensus emerges** - no oracle needed
5. **Settlement happens automatically** - payouts based on final prices

The magic is in step 4: When people with real money at stake stop changing their positions, that *is* the consensus. The market has decided.

## What This Enables

**Every online argument with stakes:**
- Anime debates, food preferences, tech opinions, sports arguments
- Culture wars, moral questions, taste battles, lifestyle choices
- Gaming debates, music taste wars, movie rankings

**Exposing false conviction:**
- When you ask "Will you bet on that?" everything changes
- Performative takes collapse
- True beliefs become measurable

**Making the internet accountable:**
- Bad faith costs real money
- Trolls pay for their claims
- Being right beats being loud

## What Makes It Possible

Two key innovations work together:

**1. Mathematical Convergence**
The AMM has a beautiful property: as liquidity grows, prices naturally stabilize. This creates favorable conditions for consensus emergence. Oracle-free settlement through natural price stability.

**2. Dynamic Evolution**
Unlike fixed-outcome markets, opinions can compete for inclusion. Weak perspectives get replaced by stronger ones. The debate evolves as understanding deepens.

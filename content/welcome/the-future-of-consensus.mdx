# The Future of Consensus

Imagine a world where any debate - no matter how subjective - can be transformed into a transparent, liquid market. Where opinions aren't just voiced but traded. Where consensus emerges naturally from the collective intelligence of participants.

**This is Trigger Market.**

## A New Primitive for Human Coordination

Throughout history, humans have struggled to aggregate opinions on subjective questions. Should the organization invest in project A or B? Which technical approach is most promising? What deserves funding?

Traditional methods are broken:

| Method | Problem |
|--------|---------|
| **Voting** | Dominated by politics, no skin in the game, binary choices |
| **Committees** | Slow, bureaucratic, vulnerable to capture |
| **Surveys** | No incentive for honesty, can't capture intensity of belief |
| **Debates** | No way to measure consensus, outcomes unclear |

Trigger Market introduces a fundamentally new primitive: **consensus markets**. Markets that don't predict objective events - they *discover and measure subjective consensus* through continuous trading.

## The Vision

Picture prediction markets, but for everything. Not just "Who will win?" but "What should we do?" and "Which is better?"

**For Organizations:**
DAO governance becomes efficient. Instead of endless forum debates and snapshot votes, create a market. The organization's collective intelligence emerges through trading. Resources flow to the ideas that gain consensus.

**For Communities:**
Technical communities can resolve eternal debates. React vs Vue? Tabs vs spaces? Microservices vs monoliths? Let the market discover consensus. Passionate believers put money where their mouth is.

**For Research:**
Which research directions deserve funding? Create markets on different approaches. Watch as researchers, practitioners, and funders collectively allocate attention and resources to the most promising paths.

**For Society:**
Policy debates. Resource allocation. Strategic decisions. Any question where multiple valid perspectives exist can become a market where consensus emerges from decentralized participation.

## Why This Matters

Traditional prediction markets unlocked massive value by letting people bet on objective outcomes. But they're limited to yes/no questions with verifiable answers.

**Trigger Market removes those limitations entirely.**

The subjective questions - the ones without clear right answers - are actually *more important* than objective predictions. They're the questions organizations struggle with daily. The debates that consume communities. The decisions that shape the future.

Now, for the first time, these questions can have liquid markets. With all the benefits markets provide:

- **Price discovery** - What does the community really believe?
- **Transparency** - Everyone sees the same prices
- **Liquidity** - Enter and exit positions continuously
- **Incentive alignment** - Profit from being right
- **Skin in the game** - Beliefs have consequences

## The Breakthrough

How do markets settle without objective outcomes? How do you pay out traders when there's no oracle to determine who was "right"?

The innovation is elegant: **markets settle based on sustained price stability**. When consensus naturally emerges and prices stabilize over time, the market settles at those prices. Every opinion pays out proportionally to its final price.

No oracle needed. No subjective judgment. Just the market's own dynamics determining when consensus has formed.

This isn't a workaround - it's a feature. The settlement mechanism itself reflects the core insight: on subjective questions, the goal isn't to find THE right answer. It's to discover where consensus lies.

## What This Enables

With Trigger Market, entirely new categories of markets become possible:

**Strategic Planning Markets**
Organizations can create markets on strategic directions. "Should we focus on enterprise or consumer?" Employees and stakeholders trade based on their knowledge and conviction. The market price represents collective intelligence.

**Resource Allocation Markets**
DAOs and protocols can create markets for funding decisions. Proposals become tradeable opinions. The community's aggregate belief determines where resources flow.

**Technical Decision Markets**
Development communities can market-test technical choices. Should the protocol implement feature X? The market aggregates distributed knowledge across the ecosystem.

**Reputation Markets**
Create markets on which projects, protocols, or approaches will gain community consensus. Trade on your insights about social consensus formation.

**Cultural Markets**
Even pure preference questions become tradeable. Markets on music, art, design choices. Consensus discovery in cultural spaces.

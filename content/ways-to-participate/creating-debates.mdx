## What Makes a Great Debate

The best debates share common traits:

**✓ Timely:** About something people are discussing *right now*

**✓ Contentious:** Real disagreement exists. Multiple valid perspectives.

**✓ Community-Relevant:** Matters to a specific community you can reach

**✓ Clear Framing:** The question is obvious. Options are distinct.

**✓ Share-Worthy:** People want to share their position and debate publicly

**Bad debates:** "What's the best color?" Generic, boring, no stakes.

**Good debates:** "Best React state management library 2025?" - Timely, dev community cares, real technical disagreement, people have strong opinions.

## How to Create a Debate

### Step 1: Pick Your Topic

Think about debates happening in communities you're part of:

**In crypto:** Which L2 will dominate? Best DeFi protocol? Top AI agent platform?

**In tech:** Framework wars. Architecture decisions. Tool choices.

**In communities:** Governance decisions. Resource allocation. Strategic direction.

**The insight:** You don't need novel topics. You need topics *people are already debating*. You're just giving them a market to express their beliefs.

### Step 2: Frame the Question

**Your debate title should be:**
- Clear and specific
- Neutral (not biased toward one option)
- Interesting enough to make people click

**Good examples:**
- "Best Ethereum L2 for DeFi: 2025"
- "React State Management: Which Library Wins?"
- "AI Safety: Which Approach Matters Most?"

**Bad examples:**
- "What do you think?" (too vague)
- "Why X is obviously better than Y" (biased)
- "Random question about stuff" (not interesting)

### Step 3: Define 3-10 Initial Opinions

Your debate needs starting opinions. Pick **3 to 10** (hard cap: 10).

**Why 10 max?** User experience. More than 10 opinions becomes cluttered and confusing. The market stays focused on the real contenders.

**How to choose initial opinions:**
- Main perspectives everyone knows about
- Most popular existing solutions
- Clear alternatives people actually debate

**You don't need to be comprehensive.** Others can propose new opinions later. You're seeding the market, not defining it forever.

**Example: "Best React State Library 2025"**
- Redux (classic)
- Zustand (minimalist)
- Jotai (atomic)
- TanStack Query (server state)

**Pro tip:** Including 1-2 "underdog" opinions can create interesting dynamics. People love backing underdogs.

### Step 4: Add Initial Liquidity

Minimum: **$50 USDC** (configurable by governance, but $50 is typical)

This liquidity gets distributed **equally across all starting opinions**. So if you create 5 opinions with $50:
- Each opinion starts with $10 liquidity
- All prices start equal (each around 0.45 in this case)
- Market opens immediately

**Why you need liquidity:** So people can trade immediately. Empty markets don't work.

**Your liquidity isn't a donation.** You receive shares proportional to your liquidity. You own part of each opinion. You can sell those shares anytime.

**Smart move:** Add more liquidity than the minimum. More liquid markets attract more traders. More trades = more fees for you.

### Step 5: Deploy on Base

Trigger Market runs on **Base** - Ethereum's L2. This means:
- Cheap gas (usually $0.10-0.50 to create a debate)
- Fast confirmations (2 seconds)
- Same security as Ethereum

Click "Create Debate" → MetaMask pops up → Confirm transaction → Your debate is live in seconds.

**That's it.** Debate deployed. Trading active. Fees start accumulating.

## Maximizing Your Debate's Success

**1. Launch with momentum:** Post about it immediately. Share in relevant communities. Get initial traders excited.

**2. Seed good discussions:** Add thoughtful comments. Explain each opinion. Give people context.

**3. Engage participants:** Respond to comments. Answer questions. Be present in your debate.

**4. Promote strategically:** Don't spam. Share where relevant. Communities hate spam but love interesting debates.

**5. Track and iterate:** See which debates succeed. Learn the patterns. Apply to future debates.

## The Portfolio Approach

Don't bet everything on one debate. Create a portfolio:

**Your first month:**
- Create 5-10 debates on topics you know
- See which themes get traction
- Learn what your communities care about
- Some will flop. Some will hit.

**Your strategy:**
- 70% proven topics (things people definitely debate)
- 20% trending topics (catching waves)
- 10% experimental (testing new ideas)

**The goal:** 2-3 hits out of 10 debates covers the misses and generates meaningful income.

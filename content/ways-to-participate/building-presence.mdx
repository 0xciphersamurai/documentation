# Building Your Presence

Anyone can make one trade. One debate. One opinion.

**The real opportunity is building lasting presence** - becoming known, building reputation, and creating compounding value over time.

This is the long game.

## Why Presence Matters

**In traditional platforms:** Your participation disappears. Comments get buried. Contributions forgotten.

**In consensus markets:** Everything you do is permanent, on-chain, and builds your identity.

**What accumulates:**
- Track record of backing correct consensus
- Debates you created that thrived
- Opinions you proposed that mattered
- Reputation as someone who contributes value

**The compounding effect:** People start watching what you back, trusting your debates, supporting your proposals.

## The Three Pillars

### **1. Quality Backing**

**Not just participating - participating well.**

**What this looks like:**
- You back opinions in your domain of expertise
- Your win rate is above average
- Your positions tend to align with eventual consensus
- You change your mind publicly when wrong (shows intellectual honesty)

**What it builds:**
- Reputation as someone who reads consensus well
- Others start watching your positions
- Your backing becomes a signal to the market

**How to build it:**
- Focus on topics you actually understand
- Don't back everything - be selective
- Explain your reasoning in comments
- Track your own performance honestly

### **2. Creating Valuable Debates**

**Not just any debates - debates that matter.**

**What this looks like:**
- Your debates get high participation
- They address questions communities actually care about
- Thoughtful arguments happen in your debates
- Your debates become reference points

**What it builds:**
- Identity as someone who launches important conversations
- Platform for your community's key debates
- Recurring audience for your debate series
- Passive income from successful debates

**How to build it:**
- Launch debates on topics with real community interest
- Seed initial discussions with thoughtful comments
- Promote to relevant communities (not spam)
- Create series that people expect and return to

### **3. Proposing Insightful Opinions**

**Not spam proposals - genuinely missing perspectives.**

**What this looks like:**
- Your proposals fill real gaps in debates
- They're well-argued and substantive
- They graduate to big league at high rates
- Community appreciates perspectives you add

**What it builds:**
- Reputation as someone who spots what others miss
- Income from successful opinion proposals
- Influence over which perspectives get debated
- Known as a contributor, not just consumer

**How to build it:**
- Only propose when you see genuine gap
- Write thorough, compelling descriptions
- Promote thoughtfully to get initial stakes
- Engage with people discussing your proposal

## The Reputation Flywheel

**Month 1: Unknown**
- You're new
- Nobody knows you
- Your actions have no reputation boost

**Month 3: Noticed**
- You've backed several debates correctly
- Created a couple successful debates
- Proposed a few good opinions
- People start recognizing your username

**Month 6: Recognized**
- Clear track record visible
- Your backing signals quality
- Your debates launch with built-in audience
- Your proposals get early support from your reputation

**Month 12: Established**
- Known contributor in your domains
- People seek your positions
- Your debates are venues of record
- Your proposals taken seriously immediately

**The flywheel:** Early quality → Small reputation → Easier to get attention → More success → Bigger reputation → Even easier → Compounds forever.

## Consistency Over Intensity

**Don't:** Intense burst for a week, then disappear.

**Do:** Steady, quality participation over months.

**The pattern:**
- Back 1-2 new debates per week (if you have conviction)
- Create 1-2 new debates per month (on important topics)
- Propose opinions when you spot gaps (quality over quantity)
- Comment thoughtfully on debates you care about

**This builds presence sustainably.** Marathons, not sprints.

## Domain Expertise

**The fastest path to presence: Become known in one domain.**

**Instead of:** Participating in everything (diluted, no depth)

**Do:** Deep dive into your area of expertise

**Examples:**
- DeFi protocols → Back DeFi debates, create DeFi debates, become THE DeFi person
- Framework discussions → Focus on framework debates, establish expertise there
- DAO governance → Specialize in governance debates, known for that domain

**Why this works:**
Communities remember specialists. "For DeFi debates, check what [username] is backing."

**Once established in one domain:** Expand to adjacent areas. Reputation transfers.

## Public Intellectual Honesty

**Being wrong isn't shameful - being dishonest is.**

**When you're wrong:**
- Acknowledge it publicly
- Explain what you learned
- Exit the position cleanly
- Apply lessons to future positions

**What this builds:**
- Trust
- Perception that you're seeking truth, not just profit
- Intellectual credibility
- Stronger reputation than pretending you're never wrong

**Example:**
"I backed X heavily, but after reading the arguments and seeing the evidence, I was wrong. Exiting my position and backing Y instead. Here's what changed my mind..."

**This signal:** You're genuine. You update on evidence. You're trustworthy.

## Creating Content Beyond the Platform

**Your presence doesn't have to stay contained:**

**Twitter/X:**
- Share your debate positions
- Explain your reasoning
- Highlight interesting debates
- Bring external audience to platform

**Blog posts / Thread:**
- Deep dives on why you back certain opinions
- Post-mortem on debates (what happened, why)
- Analysis of consensus formation patterns

**Community forums:**
- Discuss debates happening on platform
- Explain mechanisms to newcomers
- Promote quality debates

**What this builds:**
- Broader visibility
- Educator reputation
- Traffic to your debates
- Thought leadership

- Debates created → You built platform content

## Measuring Your Presence

**Track record:**
- Win rate on backed opinions
- Volume on debates you created
- Adoption rate of opinions you proposed

**Social indicators:**
- Do people mention you in comments?
- Do your positions get referenced?
- Do people wait to see what you back before deciding?

**Economic results:**
- Earnings from backing
- Passive income from created debates
- Fees from proposed opinions

**Reputation signals:**
- Username recognition
- Followers (if platform adds this)
- Being invited to closed beta of new features

The debate creator picked the starting opinions. But they didn't think of everything.

## The Opportunity

Every popular debate eventually needs new opinions:

**New frameworks emerge.** "Best React Library 2025" launched with Redux, Zustand, Jotai. Then Signals got hot. Someone proposes "Signals" opinion. It enters the AMM. They earn fees on all Signals trading.

**The pattern:** See what's missing. Propose it before others do. Earn when it succeeds.

## How Opinion Fees Work

When you propose an opinion that makes it into the AMM, you earn **0.25%** of all trading volume in that opinion.

**The math:**
- Your opinion gets $10K trading volume → You earn $25
- $100K volume → $250
- $1M volume → $2,500

**This stacks across multiple opinions.** Propose 10 opinions that each hit $100K volume? That's $2,500 in fees from opinions you spotted.

**You don't need to create the debate.** Just add the missing opinion. The debate creator earns 0.25%, you earn 0.25%, platform gets the rest (when enabled).

## How to Propose an Opinion

### Step 1: Find a Debate That's Missing Something

Browse active debates. Look for gaps:

**Missing the hot new option:** Framework/protocol/project that launched recently and has buzz but isn't in the debate yet.

**Missing the contrarian view:** Everyone's bullish on A, B, C. But there's a growing "none of them" or "D is actually better" perspective forming.

**Missing the technical angle:** Debate is high-level but there's a technical solution everyone in deep circles knows about.

**Missing the obvious:** Sometimes debate creators just forget things. "Wait, how is X not an option here?"

### Step 2: Write Your Opinion

Click "Propose Opinion" on the debate page.

**Your opinion needs:**
- **Title:** Clear, concise name (e.g., "Signals.js")
- **Description:** Explain the perspective (up to 10,000 characters)
- **Why it matters:** Give people context

**Good opinion descriptions:**
- Explain what makes this perspective unique
- Why it deserves to be in the debate
- What supporters believe

**Bad opinion descriptions:**
- "This is the best one" (not informative)
- "Idk, just adding this" (lazy)
- Pure shilling with no substance

**Pro tip:** Include relevant links, data, examples. People need to understand what they're staking on.

### Step 3: Add Your Initial Stake

Minimum: **$10** (typical, configurable by governance)

Your stake puts your money where your mouth is. You believe this opinion deserves to be in the AMM.

**Where your stake goes:** Into the "baby league" pool for this opinion. Others can add stakes too. When the total reaches the threshold, anyone can trigger graduation to the big league (AMM).

**The threshold:** Roughly 5% more than the weakest opinion currently in the AMM. This ensures your candidate has real support before replacing an existing option.

### Step 4: Promote Your Opinion

You proposed it. Now get others to stake on it.

**Share in relevant communities:** "Just proposed [Opinion] in [Debate] - here's why it matters..."

**Make your case:** Comment on the debate. Explain why this perspective is important.

**Get believers to stake:** Each stake brings it closer to the threshold. Show the progress bar. Create FOMO.

**Example:** "Opinion X is at $85 of $105 needed. Only $20 more to get into the AMM!"

### Step 5: Watch It Graduate

Once your opinion reaches the threshold (typically 105% of the weakest AMM opinion's value), anyone can trigger the graduation:

**What happens:**
- Weakest opinion drops to baby league (automatically determined)
- Your opinion enters the big league AMM
- All stakes convert to tradeable shares
- Trading begins immediately
- You start earning 0.25% of all trades in your opinion

**Your shares:** If you staked $50 out of $105 total, you own ~47.6% of shares in your opinion. You can hold, trade, or sell them - separate from your fee earnings.

## Maximizing Your Opinion's Volume

You earn fees based on trading volume in your opinion. Here's how to maximize it:

### 1. Pick Opinions That Will Get Traction

**Look for:**
- Recent momentum (people are already talking about it)
- Strong communities (built-in audience)
- Real differentiation (not just "option X but slightly different")
- Timing (catching a wave vs fighting uphill)

**Avoid:**
- Dead projects no one cares about
- Tiny niche with no trading interest
- Obvious duplicates of existing opinions
- Pure meme picks with no substance

### 2. Make It Discoverable

**Great descriptions matter.** When someone browses candidate opinions, yours needs to be clear and compelling.

**Use keywords.** If searching for "Svelte", your "Svelte 5 with Runes" opinion should mention Svelte prominently.

**Add context.** Links to docs, GitHub stars, community size - give traders confidence.

### 3. Build Early Support

**The first few stakes matter most.** They signal: "This is real, not random spam."

**Coordinate ethically:** Share with communities who care. If proposing a Rust framework, tell Rust devs. They'll stake if it's genuinely good.

**Don't fake it:** Wash trading with your own alt accounts is obvious and hurts credibility.

### 4. Engage After Graduation

**Your opinion made it to the AMM - don't disappear.**

**Comment on price movements.** "Opinion X hit 0.30 - momentum building!"

**Share updates.** If your opinion is about a project and that project ships something major, comment about it.

**Defend against FUD.** If people spread misinfo about your opinion, correct it with facts.

**Your presence keeps it relevant.** Relevant opinions get more trading volume.

## The Timing Game

**Early proposals = more upside, more risk**

Propose an opinion in a debate's first week? You might be early to something that explodes. Or you might be adding noise to an unsettled market.

**Late proposals = catching waves**

Propose an opinion in week 3 when clear momentum exists? Lower risk, still good upside if you're early to a narrative shift.

**Post-settlement proposals**

Wait, debates that settled can still have new opinions proposed? No - settled markets freeze. But debates close to settlement rarely accept new opinions anyway (no time for them to trade much).

**Optimal window:** Propose when debate has momentum (not brand new) but before consensus fully forms (not about to settle).

## Multi-Opinion Strategy

Don't propose one opinion and pray. Build a portfolio:

**Month 1:** Propose 10 opinions across different debates
- 3 make it to AMM
- You earn fees on all 3

**Month 3:** Proposed 30 opinions total
- 8 in AMM
- Some hit high volume, some are quiet
- Portfolio effect smooths variance

**Month 6:** Proposed 60+ opinions
- 15-20 in AMM
- Multiple winners
- Steady fee income

**The insight:** Each proposal is a call option. Some expire worthless (never reach threshold). Some print (massive volume). Portfolio of calls = expected positive return.

## What If Your Opinion Gets Replaced?

**It happens.** Opinions cycle in and out of the AMM.

**Your fee earnings:** Already earned fees are yours forever. Claim them anytime.

**Future fees:** If your opinion drops to baby league, you don't earn fees while it's there (no trading happening in baby league).

**If it returns:** Comes back to AMM? You start earning fees again on new volume.

**The lifecycle:** Your opinion can go: Baby → Big → Baby → Big → Baby → Big → Settled. You earn fees every time it's in the big league.

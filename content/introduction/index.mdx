# Introduction

Welcome to Trigger Market documentation. This section covers the fundamentals of consensus markets and why they matter.

## What You'll Learn

- **What is Trigger Market?** — How we turn subjective debates into markets with real stakes
- **Why It Matters** — The problem with online discourse and how skin in the game fixes it
- **How It Works** — A detailed walkthrough of the consensus market mechanism

## Quick Overview

Trigger Market is the first **consensus market** platform. Unlike prediction markets that need oracles to verify objective outcomes, consensus markets work for subjective debates where the collective agreement of participants *is* the resolution.

Every online argument—from "best programming language" to "pineapple on pizza"—can now have stakes. When you have to put money on your opinion, performative takes collapse and true beliefs emerge.

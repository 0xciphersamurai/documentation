# Welcome to Trigger Market

Imagine being able to create a market on any debate—even questions without objectively "correct" answers. Questions like "Which framework is best for our project?" or "What should our community prioritize?"

Traditional prediction markets can't handle these. They need external oracles to determine outcomes, limiting them to objective events with verifiable results.

**Trigger Market changes this.** It's a platform for trading on subjective debates, using price stability itself as the settlement mechanism. When participants reach consensus and prices stabilize, the market settles—no oracle needed.

## The Vision

Every organization faces decisions where multiple perspectives have merit. Technical teams debate architectural choices. DAOs discuss resource allocation. Communities evaluate strategic directions. Research groups prioritize approaches.

These debates involve legitimate disagreement. There's no objectively "right" answer to discover—consensus itself is the answer.

Trigger Market provides infrastructure for these discussions to become markets. Participants stake capital on the perspectives they believe will gain consensus. Prices evolve as the debate unfolds. When stability emerges, settlement occurs automatically.

This creates something new: **consensus markets**. Markets that don't predict future events but rather discover and measure collective agreement on subjective questions.

## The Core Innovation

Three mechanisms work together to make oracle-free consensus markets possible:

**1. L² Norm AMM**
An automated market maker that prices opinions using elegant mathematics. As liquidity accumulates, prices naturally become more stable, creating favorable conditions for settlement.

**2. Dynamic Opinion Replacement**
Opinions aren't fixed at market creation. New perspectives can be proposed and, if they gather support, can replace existing opinions. Markets evolve as understanding deepens.

**3. Time-Weighted Settlement**
Markets settle when prices remain stable over time. The system tracks price stability using exponentially-weighted moving averages, ensuring that brief volatility doesn't prevent settlement but sustained consensus does trigger it.

## What This Enables

**For DAOs**: Create markets on governance proposals. Let stakeholders trade on different strategic directions. Price discovery reveals collective preference.

**For Technical Communities**: Settle recurring debates. "Best practice" discussions become tradeable markets. Consensus emerges economically.

**For Research**: Aggregate expert opinion on open questions. Where should resources flow? What approaches seem most promising? Markets provide continuous signals.

**For Organizations**: Decision support through collective intelligence. Strategic choices, resource allocation, priority setting—all can become consensus markets.

## Who Participates

**Traders** buy and sell shares of different opinions. If you believe an opinion will gain consensus, you stake capital on it. When the market settles, you receive payouts proportional to final prices.

**Debate Creators** launch markets on questions their communities care about. They earn 0.25% of all trading volume that ever occurs in debates they create.

**Opinion Proposers** surface perspectives that might be missing from existing debates. When their opinions get adopted into the active market, they earn 0.25% of trading volume involving those opinions.

**Communities** use the platform for collective decision-making, priority setting, and consensus formation on questions that matter to them.

## How It Works (Simple Version)

1. **Someone creates a debate** with initial opinions (e.g., "Best approach: A, B, C, D")
2. **Participants trade** on opinions they believe will gain consensus
3. **Prices adjust** based on buying and selling activity
4. **New opinions can be proposed** and replace existing ones if they gather support
5. **When prices stabilize** for long enough, settlement triggers automatically
6. **Payouts occur** based on final prices and share holdings

No oracle is consulted. No external authority determines the outcome. The market's own dynamics—observable price stability—indicate consensus formation.

## Built Fully Onchain

Trigger Market deploys on Base, an Ethereum L2. This provides:

- **Permissionless participation** - Anyone can create markets, propose opinions, and trade
- **Censorship resistance** - No central authority can shut down debates
- **Transparent settlement** - All logic executes onchain, verifiable by anyone
- **Low transaction costs** - Base's efficiency makes frequent trading practical
- **Composability** - Other protocols can integrate with consensus markets

The frontend (built with Next.js) is simply a convenient interface. All core functionality lives in smart contracts. You can interact with the protocol directly if you prefer.

## Why Now

Three things make this possible:

**Mathematical Foundation**: The L² norm AMM provides the convergence properties needed for stability-based settlement. Price sensitivity decreases cubically with liquidity, enabling natural consensus formation.

**Blockchain Infrastructure**: Base and other L2s now offer the combination of decentralization, low costs, and performance needed for prediction market platforms.

**Market Readiness**: The DeFi ecosystem has matured. Users understand wallets, trading interfaces, and onchain applications. The infrastructure is ready for consensus markets.

## What Makes This Special

Traditional voting is dominated by politics and has no cost to poor judgment. Forums aggregate opinions but can't measure intensity or confidence. Committees are slow and vulnerable to capture.

Consensus markets combine the best of prediction markets (economic incentives, skin in the game, price discovery) with applicability to subjective questions (no oracle dependency, dynamic evolution, stability-based settlement).

**This is genuinely new.** Not an iteration on existing prediction markets, but a different category of coordination infrastructure.

# What is Trigger Market?

Trigger Market is a new kind of prediction market—one that doesn't need oracles or objective events to settle. Instead, settlement emerges from observable consensus formation among participants.

## The Oracle Problem

Traditional prediction markets have worked brilliantly for objective questions:

- "Will Bitcoin reach $100K by December?"
- "Who will win the election?"
- "Will the Fed raise rates next month?"

These work because there's an objective event that eventually happens, and an oracle (trusted source) can verify what happened.

**But this creates a fundamental limitation:** You can only create markets for questions with objective, verifiable answers.

## The Subjective Gap

Many of the most important questions we face don't have objective answers:

- **In DAOs**: "Which proposal should we fund?"
- **In research**: "Which approach is most promising?"
- **In communities**: "What principles should guide us?"
- **In technology**: "Which framework best solves this problem?"

These are **debates**, not predictions. Reasonable people disagree. There's no external event that resolves them. Yet these questions desperately need collective decision-making tools.

Traditional prediction markets simply can't handle them.

## The Breakthrough

**What if consensus formation *is* the resolution?**

When people with real money at stake reach equilibrium—when prices stop changing because everyone has expressed their view—that equilibrium *is* the answer.

Not because an authority declared it. Because the market, through economic commitment, revealed it.

## How Trigger Market Works

### 1. Create a Debate

Anyone can create a debate with initial opinions:

**Example Debate**: "Best React state management in 2025?"

**Initial Opinions**:
- Redux
- Zustand
- Jotai
- MobX

### 2. Trade on Opinions

Participants buy and sell shares in different opinions. Prices adjust based on supply and demand:

- More buying → Price increases
- More selling → Price decreases

Prices reflect collective belief weighted by capital allocation.

### 3. Market Evolves

Unlike traditional markets where outcomes are fixed, **opinions compete for inclusion**:

- Anyone can propose new opinions (like "XState")
- New opinions accumulate stake while in the "candidate pool"
- When a candidate has enough stake, it can **replace** the weakest active opinion
- Replaced opinions don't disappear—they return to the candidate pool and can re-enter later

This creates **evolutionary markets** where perspectives compete based on demonstrated support.

### 4. Consensus Emerges

As the debate matures:

- Arguments have been made
- Capital has been allocated
- Prices naturally stabilize around equilibrium values

When prices remain stable over time (meeting specific mathematical criteria), **the market has reached consensus**.

### 5. Settlement Happens Automatically

No oracle needed. The system detects price stability through four criteria:

1. **Minimum age**: Market must be at least 7 days old (configurable)
2. **Low variance**: Prices must be historically stable
3. **Current agreement**: Recent prices must match historical averages
4. **Multiple confirmations**: Stability must persist across multiple checks

When all four criteria are met, the market settles automatically. Final prices determine payouts.

<Callout title="The Key Insight" type="info">
Price stability = consensus reached. When participants with skin in the game stop changing their positions, that equilibrium state *is* the answer to the debate.
</Callout>

## What Makes It Possible

Two innovations working together:

### Mathematical Convergence

The L² norm AMM (Automated Market Maker) has a special property: **as liquidity grows, prices become progressively more stable**.

Technically, the Hessian (measure of price sensitivity) decreases cubically with total liquidity. In plain English: mature markets naturally resist price manipulation, creating conditions where consensus can emerge.

You don't need to understand the math. Just know it makes oracle-free settlement mathematically possible.

### Dynamic Opinion Competition

Opinions enter and exit the market based on demonstrated support. This lets debates evolve as understanding deepens:

- Initial framings can be inadequate
- New perspectives emerge
- Irrelevant options get replaced
- The market adapts naturally

## What This Enables

**Governance Markets**: DAOs can create markets for proposal evaluation, resource allocation, strategic direction.

**Research Consensus**: Scientific communities can aggregate expert opinion on technical questions, research priorities.

**Collective Decisions**: Any community can resolve debates through economic commitment rather than voting.

**Subjective Forecasting**: Questions about future trends where there's no binary outcome, just competing perspectives.

## The Vision

A world where:

- **Any debate can have a market** - Permissionless creation
- **Consensus emerges economically** - Not from authority, but from participants
- **Truth-seeking is incentivized** - You profit by supporting positions that achieve consensus
- **No censorship** - Fully onchain on Base, unstoppable
- **Complete transparency** - All settlement logic visible and verifiable

This is infrastructure for collective intelligence on subjective questions.

## Built Fully Onchain

Everything happens on Base (Ethereum L2):

- **Smart contracts** hold all funds and execute all logic
- **Settlement** determined by observable on-chain price history
- **No multisigs** controlling outcomes
- **Permissionless** - anyone can participate, create, propose
- **Transparent** - anyone can verify

The frontend (built with Next.js) is just a convenient interface. You could build your own. The blockchain is the source of truth.

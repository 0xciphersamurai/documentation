# How It Works

Let's walk through the complete lifecycle of a consensus market, from creation to settlement, with concrete examples.

## The Complete Journey

<Steps>

<Step>
### Someone Creates a Debate

**Alex wants to start a market** about the best React state management library in 2025.

**They provide:**
- A title and description
- 3-10 initial opinions (let's say 4: Redux, Zustand, Jotai, MobX)
- Initial liquidity (minimum $50 USDC, distributed equally across all opinions)

**What happens:**
1. Smart contract deploys on Base blockchain
2. Each opinion starts with equal amounts: $12.50 worth each
3. Initial prices are equal: each opinion at 50% (prices satisfy $\sum_i p_i^2 = 1$)
4. Market opens immediately for trading
5. Alex receives shares proportional to their initial liquidity

**Cost:** ~$0.50 gas fee + minimum $50 USDC liquidity
</Step>

<Step>
### People Start Trading

**Morgan discovers the market** and believes Zustand is undervalued.

**Current prices:**
- Redux: 52% → Zustand: 48% → Jotai: 45% → MobX: 43%

Morgan thinks Zustand should be higher. They buy $100 worth of Zustand shares.

**What happens:**
1. Morgan's $100 goes into the market vault
2. They receive shares in Zustand (quantity calculated by the AMM formula)
3. Zustand's price increases (exact amount depends on market size and AMM math)
4. All other prices adjust downward slightly (they compete for the price "space")
5. Morgan's position is now visible in their profile

**Key insight:** Every trade moves prices. More buying = higher price. More selling = lower price. Prices reflect collective belief weighted by capital.
</Step>

<Step>
### A New Opinion Emerges

**Riley notices XState is missing** from the debate. They decide to propose it.

**Riley provides:**
- A description of XState and why it matters
- Initial stake of $25 USDC

**What happens:**
1. XState enters the "candidate pool"—it's not in the main market yet
2. Riley owns shares representing 100% of the $25 stake
3. Other people can add stake to XState if they agree it should be included
4. A progress bar shows: "$25 / $78 needed to replace weakest opinion"

**The requirement:** To enter the main market, XState needs stake $\geq 105\%$ of the weakest active opinion's value.

(The 5\% buffer prevents opinions from immediately replacing each other back and forth.)

**Current weakest opinion:** MobX has vault value of $75. So XState needs $78.75 to be eligible for replacement (105\% of $75).
</Step>

<Step>
### Opinions Get Replaced

**More people stake on XState.** Total stake reaches $80.

**Jordan notices** and clicks "Replace Weakest Opinion"

**What happens:**
1. Contract identifies the weakest opinion (MobX with $75 vault value)
2. **MobX exits the AMM:**
   - All MobX shareholders' shares now represent claims on $75 candidate stake
   - MobX becomes a candidate with $75 in its pool
   - Previous shareholders can unstake (sell for USDC minus 1% fee) or hold hoping for re-entry
3. **XState enters the AMM:**
   - The $80 stake converts to AMM shares using closed-form formula
   - All XState stakers receive proportional AMM shares
   - XState now has a price and trades like other active opinions
4. Users get notifications about the swap

**Crucial detail:** User share counts don't change on-chain. Only the *interpretation* changes:
- Before: "You have 10 shares in MobX (worth $8 at current price)"
- After: "You have $7.50 staked in candidate MobX" (your 10 shares now represent 10\% of $75 candidate stake)

This is the **proportional claims system**—your ownership percentage stays the same, but what you own has changed state.
</Step>

<Step>
### Market Matures and Prices Stabilize

**Weeks pass.** People have:
- Researched the libraries
- Shared their experiences
- Made their cases
- Allocated their capital

**Price evolution:**
- Week 1: High volatility as opinions form
- Week 2: Slower movement as arguments are made
- Week 3: Prices settle around consensus values

**Current prices (stable for days):**
- Zustand: 38%
- Redux: 32%
- XState: 18%
- Jotai: 12%

The system tracks this stability using exponential moving averages (EWMA). It's measuring: "Are prices still changing meaningfully, or has everyone reached their position?"
</Step>

<Step>
### Settlement Criteria Are Met

**After 3 weeks**, the system detects all four criteria:

1. ✅ **Minimum age**: 21 days (required: 7 days)
2. ✅ **Low variance**: Price variance < 0.0001 threshold
3. ✅ **Current agreement**: Prices within 2% of historical averages
4. ✅ **Multiple confirmations**: Stability held for 5+ consecutive checks (2.5+ hours)

**Settlement triggers automatically:**
- Market freezes immediately
- No more trades possible
- Final prices locked in
- 100-block confirmation period begins (~20 minutes)

**What this means:** The market has reached consensus. Prices stopped changing because participants expressed their views and reached equilibrium.
</Step>

<Step>
### Users Claim Payouts

**After the 100-block confirmation period**, payouts become available.

**Morgan's position:**
- Bought Zustand for $100 when price was 48%
- Received ~208 shares (formula: $\Delta Q = -q + \sqrt{q^2 + 2MC + M^2}$)
- Final price: 38%

**Morgan's payout:**
- Shares × Final price = 208 × 0.38 = $79.04
- Minus 1% fee = $78.25
- Result: Lost $21.75 (bought high, price fell)

**Riley's position:**
- Staked $25 on XState as candidate
- XState entered AMM and settled at 18%
- Riley held through settlement

**Riley's payout:**
- Their proportional shares × final price
- Made profit because XState went from candidate to 18% of final consensus

**Important:** Payout claims have **NO deadline**. You can claim anytime after the 100-block finality period. You'll never lose your earned payouts.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Key Mechanics Explained

### The AMM (Automated Market Maker)

The market uses a special pricing formula that enables oracle-free consensus.

**What you need to know:**
- Prices automatically normalize to valid ranges
- As liquidity grows, prices become naturally more stable
- All trades are calculated instantly
- The math creates conditions where consensus *can* emerge

**What you don't need to know:**
- The detailed formulas (unless you're curious—see Advanced section)
- The underlying mathematics
- The mathematical proofs

**The key property:** Price sensitivity decreases cubically as liquidity grows. Mature markets are naturally resistant to manipulation.

### The Opinion Lifecycle

Opinions exist in two states:

**ACTIVE (in the AMM):**
- Has a quantity and price
- People buy/sell shares
- Trades on the main market
- Contributes to settlement tracking

**CANDIDATE (in the pool):**
- Has accumulated stake
- People add/withdraw stake
- Waiting to enter AMM
- Doesn't affect settlement

**The cycle:**
```
Proposed as Candidate
    ↓
Accumulates stake
    ↓
Replaces weakest active opinion → Becomes ACTIVE
    ↓
Trading happens, prices evolve
    ↓
Gets replaced by stronger candidate → Becomes CANDIDATE
    ↓
Can accumulate stake and re-enter...
```

Opinions cycle through states indefinitely. They never disappear. An opinion replaced today might return next month with renewed support.

### Settlement Detection

The system tracks price stability automatically using EWMA (Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages).

**How it works:**
- Every trade updates the statistics (if 30+ minutes passed since last update)
- Prices are weighted by how long they persisted
- A 10-minute price spike contributes negligible weight
- Days of stability dominate the statistics

**The four criteria** (all required):

1. **Minimum age**: Prevents premature settlement (default: 7 days, set by creator)
2. **Low variance**: Historical price stability (threshold: 0.0001)
3. **Current agreement**: Recent prices match long-term average (within 2%)
4. **Multiple confirmations**: Stability persisted across 5+ checks (2.5+ hours)

When all four are met → settlement triggers → no more trades → payouts calculated

<Callout title="Why These Criteria" type="info">
Each criterion defends against a different attack:
- Minimum age: prevents instant manipulation
- Low variance: ensures genuine stability, not momentary calm
- Current agreement: prevents last-second price manipulation
- Multiple confirmations: ensures stability is sustained, not lucky timing
</Callout>

### Fee Structure

**Total: 1% on exits only** (selling, unstaking, claiming payouts)

**Distribution when all fees enabled:**
- 0.5% → Platform treasury
- 0.25% → Debate creator (earned on all trading forever)
- 0.25% → Opinion creator (earned when their opinion trades)

**Current state:**
- Platform fees: Disabled (0%)
- Creator fees: Enabled (0.5% total)
- Can be toggled by governance

**Important deadlines:**
- **Fee claims**: Must be claimed within 365 days of settlement
- **Payout claims**: NO deadline—claim anytime

## What Makes It Work

Three elements together:

**1. Economic Commitment**

Capital is at stake. This creates:
- Sybil resistance (manipulation costs real money)
- Information revelation (people research before risking capital)
- Truth-seeking incentives (profit by being right)

**2. Mathematical Convergence**

The AMM naturally facilitates stability:
- Price sensitivity decreases as liquidity grows
- Mature markets resist manipulation
- Settlement can emerge without external triggers

**3. Dynamic Evolution**

Opinions compete and evolve:
- Better perspectives can enter
- Weak ones get replaced
- The market adapts to new information
- Debates don't ossify around initial framings

# Why It Matters

Trigger Market isn't just another prediction market platform. It's infrastructure for a new kind of collective intelligence - one that works for questions traditional markets can't touch.

## The Limits of Traditional Prediction Markets

Prediction markets have proven remarkably effective at forecasting objective events. But they've remained limited to a narrow set of questions because they require:

### External Resolution

Someone or something must declare what happened. This creates:
- **Centralization points**: Who do you trust?
- **Manipulation risk**: Oracles can be corrupted
- **Edge case ambiguity**: What if the event is unclear?
- **Operational overhead**: Running oracles is expensive

### Objective Outcomes

Markets need discrete, verifiable results. This excludes:
- Value judgments ("What's the best approach?")
- Ongoing debates ("Which framework is superior?")
- Complex evaluations ("Is this research promising?")
- Philosophical questions ("What should we prioritize?")

**Result**: Huge classes of important questions remain unaddressed by market mechanisms.

## What Trigger Market Unlocks

### 1. Subjective Consensus Formation

For the first time, you can create markets on questions like:

**Technology**
- "Which architecture pattern is best for microservices?"
- "Will Rust replace C++ in systems programming?"
- "Is Server Components the future of React?"

**Governance**
- "Should we fund this grant proposal?"
- "Which product feature should we prioritize?"
- "Is this protocol upgrade worth the complexity?"

**Research**
- "Which AI safety approach is most promising?"
- "Will quantum computing be practical by 2030?"
- "Is dark energy real or a modeling artifact?"

**Strategy**
- "Should we pivot to enterprise?"
- "Is now the right time to launch?"
- "Which market should we enter first?"

These questions have no objective answer, but they benefit enormously from aggregating informed opinion.

### 2. Economic Truth-Seeking

Traditional forums, polls, and voting suffer from:
- **No cost to poor judgment**: Easy to have uninformed opinions
- **Sybil attacks**: One person, many accounts
- **Social pressure**: Groupthink and bandwagons
- **No skin in the game**: Words are cheap

Trigger Market solves this by requiring participants to **put money behind their beliefs**:

### 3. Permissionless Coordination

Anyone can:
- **Create markets** on topics they care about (no approval needed)
- **Propose opinions** that others might not have considered
- **Participate globally** regardless of jurisdiction
- **Earn from contribution** (creating markets, proposing opinions, early trading)

This is coordination infrastructure that:
- **Can't be shut down** (deployed on Base, a decentralized L2)
- **Has no gatekeepers** (smart contracts enforce rules, not humans)
- **Provides transparent records** (all trades and outcomes verifiable onchain)
- **Enables programmable settlement** (DAOs can trigger actions based on results)

### 4. Dynamic Truth Discovery

Traditional markets are static: outcomes fixed at creation. Trigger Market debates **evolve**:

**Initial framing** (Day 1):
"Best state management for React?"
- Redux
- Zustand
- Jotai

**Community discovers** (Day 15):
"Wait, server state is different from client state. We need better options."

**New opinion proposed**:
- TanStack Query (for server state)

**Gets adopted**, market now reflects better understanding of the problem space.

## Real-World Applications

### DAO Governance

Instead of binary voting, create markets on strategic decisions:

**Example**: "Should Uniswap deploy on Cosmos?"

**Opinions**:
- Yes, expand immediately
- Yes, but after v4 launches
- No, focus on Ethereum L2s
- No, too much complexity

Let the market aggregate informed opinion from stakeholders, developers, and community members. The winning opinion reflects genuine consensus, not political maneuvering.

### Research Prioritization

Academic and industry research faces constant tradeoffs about where to invest effort.

**Example**: "Most promising path to AGI safety?"

**Opinions**:
- Constitutional AI (Anthropic's approach)
- Scalable oversight
- Mechanistic interpretability
- Multi-agent alignment

Researchers stake on approaches they believe in. The market provides continuous feedback on where the field thinks resources should flow.

### Corporate Strategy

Companies need ways to aggregate internal expertise without politics.

**Example**: "Best go-to-market strategy for our B2B SaaS?"

**Opinions**:
- Product-led growth
- Sales-led growth
- Partner-led growth
- Community-led growth

Employees (who know the product and market) stake on strategies they believe will work. Leadership gets unfiltered signal from those closest to reality.

### Investment Theses

Coordinate capital around shared conviction.

**Example**: "Which L2 will win 2025?"

**Opinions**:
- Optimism ecosystem
- Arbitrum ecosystem
- zkSync ecosystem
- Base ecosystem

Traders put money where their research leads them. Price evolution reveals shifting market sentiment based on developments, launches, and adoption metrics.

## Why Now?

Three technological developments make this possible:

### 1. Blockchain Infrastructure

**Base** (and other L2s) provide:
- Low transaction costs (pennies per trade)
- Fast finality (seconds, not minutes)
- Decentralized execution (no single point of failure)
- Programmable settlement (smart contracts = trustless logic)

### 2. Mathematical Foundations

Novel AMM design using L² norm cost functions enables:
- Natural price stability as markets mature
- Closed-form solutions (efficient onchain computation)
- Convergence properties (facilitating oracle-free settlement)

### 3. DeFi Maturity

The ecosystem now has:
- Established stablecoins (USDC for denominating markets)
- Wallet infrastructure (MetaMask, Rainbow, Coinbase Wallet)
- User familiarity with trading interfaces
- Liquidity to bootstrap markets

## The Vision

A world where:

- **Any debate can become a market** (no permission, no gatekeepers)
- **Consensus emerges economically** (not through authority or politics)
- **Truth-seeking is incentivized** (profit from being right)
- **Knowledge is aggregated transparently** (onchain record of collective intelligence)
- **Communities coordinate** without centralization

This is infrastructure for collective decision-making at scale.

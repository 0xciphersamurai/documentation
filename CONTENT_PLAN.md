## Tone Guidelines

### ✅ Do:
- Be enthusiastic about the vision
- Explain clearly and simply
- Show genuine excitement for the innovation
- Use confident, informative language
- Include practical examples
- Focus on understanding and participation

### ❌ Don't:
- Make return projections or promises
- Use pressure tactics ("act now or miss out")
- Over-hype with superlatives
- Suggest guaranteed profits
- Use "pump and dump" language
- Create FOMO artificially

### Good Examples:
- "Consensus markets enable something new: trading on subjective debates"
- "Early participants help shape how the platform evolves"
- "Understanding these mechanics gives you insight into how consensus forms"

### Bad Examples:
- "Extraordinary opportunities measured in multiples"
- "Every day you wait is money left on the table"
- "10x your capital with these strategies"

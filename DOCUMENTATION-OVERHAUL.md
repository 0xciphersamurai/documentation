### **Understanding Debates** (Completely New Section)

**Purpose:** Explain mechanisms high-level, user-focused, reassuring

#### **1. How Consensus Forms**

**What it covers:**
- Natural arc: Discovery → Debate → Crystallization → Settlement
- What drives opinion prices (community support, not just trading)
- Opinion lifecycle meaning (baby/big league as competition)
- "The math works" reassurance without technical details
- Reading where debates are headed

**Key messaging:**
- Consensus forms naturally over weeks
- Your participation shapes it
- Math is sound (don't worry about details)
- Social intelligence matters most

#### **2. Opinion Dynamics**

**What it covers:**
- Competition model (max 10 in big league)
- Baby → Big league graduation (what it means for users)
- Big → Baby replacement (value preserved, comebacks possible)
- The cycle (opinions can return multiple times)
- Natural selection metaphor

**Key messaging:**
- Opinions compete like ecosystem
- Weak replaced by strong
- Your shares always represent proportional value
- Comebacks happen - nothing dies permanently

#### **3. Backing What You Believe**

**What it covers:**
- Start with conviction (not profit motive)
- When to back (early/middle/late phases based on conviction)
- How much (based on belief, not portfolio theory)
- When to exit (conviction changes, not P&L targets)
- Psychological aspects (not finance stress)

**Key messaging:**
- Back what you genuinely believe
- Conviction over optimization
- Social intelligence is edge
- Profit is natural result of being right

#### **4. Reading the Room**

**What it covers:**
- Social intelligence as core skill
- What to watch (comments, who's backing, external events)
- Patterns to recognize (momentum, false starts, comebacks)
- Domain-specific reading (technical vs governance vs cultural)
- Misreading warnings

**Key messaging:**
- "Reading people, not charts"
- "Humanities majors beat finance bros"
- Immersion in communities is edge
- Social awareness > technical analysis
